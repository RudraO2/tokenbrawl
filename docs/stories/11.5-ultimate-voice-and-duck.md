# Story 11.5 — The music gets out of the way

As a visitor, I want the Ultimate to be announced and the music to duck under
it, so that it sounds as big as it looks.

## Reference material

**Read `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` first** for the reference project's location
(`<REF>` below) and the four mechanisms that may never be copied — this story
touches the wall-clock one directly.

| What | Where |
|---|---|

## The gap this closes, and why it was left open

Story 10.5 shipped `sfx_special` on the SFX bus and recorded in its completion
note that the reference fires **two** cues on the same event — `vo_ultimate` on
the voice bus as well — and that `vo()` ducks the music to **25%** for the
line's duration. Only the SFX half shipped, deliberately: the voice table was
KO-only by design and the duck was a mix decision that story had not been asked
to make.

The owner has now asked for it. Most of the perceived size of the reference's
Ultimate is the **music getting out of the way**, not the cue itself.

## The lucky number

Story 9.6's duck window is **90 clock frames**. Story 10.4's cinematic freeze is
**90 clock frames**. They were tuned independently and they are the same length,
so a voice cue placed on the cinematic's opening frame ducks the music across
exactly the cutscene and restores on the frame it ends. Worth stating, because
it means this story is a table edit rather than a new mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** the Ultimate firing, **When** its cue is emitted, **Then** a voice cue
is emitted on the voice bus on the same frame as `sfx_special`, and the music
bus ducks for the cinematic's length and restores after it.

**Given** the duck, **When** it is expressed, **Then** it is an integer count of
clock frames written into the precomputed track, never a `setTimeout` and never
`setTargetAtTime(x, ctx.currentTime, tau)` — the reference uses both and both
are banned here (INV-1, INV-3).

**Given** a visitor seeking across the Ultimate repeatedly, **When** they do,
**Then** neither cue stacks, and the music gain at a frame reached by seeking
equals the gain at the same frame reached by playing.

**Given** the KO's own voice line, **When** a KO follows an Ultimate closely,
**Then** the per-fighter rate limit still applies and the two do not talk over
each other.

**Given** a browser with audio blocked or absent, **When** the Ultimate fires,
**Then** playback is silent and otherwise identical.

**Given** any Match, **When** its Final-State Hash is compared before and after,
**Then** it is unchanged (AD-15).

**Given** the new asset, **When** it lands, **Then** `docs/ASSETS.md` records
its provenance and licence **in the same change**.

## Visual gate (Story 10.1) — run 2026-08-08

An audio story's gate is the cue verified **fetched and started on the page**,
the way Story 10.5's was run, plus the bus levels read off the real graph.

**How the state was reached.** The demo Match contains no Ultimate, so
`DEMO_REPLAY_URL` was temporarily pointed at `spectate-03`; the edit was
reverted before the commit and `git status` is clean of it. `prefers-reduced-motion`
was **not** stubbed and did not need to be: reduced motion changes what the
cinematic draws, not how long it runs, so the clock frame the cue rides is the
same either way. `requestAnimationFrame` was replaced with a queue so autoplay
could not overwrite a scrub. The transport was then driven **one frame at a
time** through the timeline input — a `+1` seek is an ordinary advance to the
director, which is the only way a one-shot fires at all.

The graph was read by overriding `AudioParam.prototype.value`'s setter and
`AudioBufferSourceNode.prototype.{connect,start}` after load. `setGains` writes
music, then SFX, then voice, every frame, so the three `AudioParam`s register in
that order and a source's destination identifies its bus. The one that went
`0.80 → 0.25` is the music bus, which anchors the mapping.

The Ultimate is at clock frame **401** of 630; caster is agent 0, connected.

**Replay player — the cue, frame by frame.**

| Frame | Sources started | On which bus | Music gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 397–400 | 0 | — | 0.80 |
| **401** | **2** | **voice, then SFX** | **0.25** |
| 402–406 | 0 | — | 0.25 |

Two sources and only two, on the one frame, in the reference's order — the
announcement first, the impact second. Nothing starts on any of the 90 frozen
holds that follow, which is the machine-gun failure the clock indexing exists to
prevent, verified against the running page rather than against the track.

**The duck, measured on the real `GainNode`.** Sweeping frames 381→520 and
recording every write to the music bus: `0.80` through 400, `0.25` from **401 to
490 inclusive — 90 writes, contiguous, no hole** — and `0.80` again from 491.
That is exactly the boundary the suite pins, and it is the whole of the ducking
in the Match: `spectate-03` carries no KO event, so the announcement is the only
voice line in it.

**The asset really was fetched.** `GET /audio/vo_ultimate.mp3` → **200**,
same-origin, and it appears in the network log *between* `sfx_hit_h.mp3` (the
heavy at clock 388) and `sfx_special.mp3` — a cue's file is only requested when
`play` is called on it, so the request is itself the evidence that the voice cue
fired, in the order it fired.

**Arcade and Spectate**, screenshotted as the epic requires even though this
story names neither: both unbroken. Spectate plays `spectate-03` with the arcade
HUD and no audio layer, which is Story 11.6's job. Arcade renders its controls
intact, Ultimate key included.

**Console**, filtered to error and warn: **no error at all**, and the single
warning is mine — the `link preload` notice caused by temporarily redirecting the
demo log. Nothing warned about a cue that failed to load, which is where a pack
that would not decode reports itself and nowhere else.

**No defect found by the gate.** Unlike Story 11.4's, which caught one the suite
could not: this story's whole surface is the cue stream and the gain stream, and
both are things a test can hold. The gate confirmed the page agrees with the
track rather than discovering it did not.
