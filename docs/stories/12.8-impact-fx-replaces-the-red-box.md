# Story 12.8 — The hit indicator stops looking like a debug hitbox

As a viewer, I want a landed hit to look like a hit, so that the most frequent event in the game is not drawn as a red wireframe rectangle that reads as leftover developer instrumentation.

## The gap, measured

`apps/web/src/render/artist.ts`, around line 185:

```ts
if (fighter.animation.clip === 'hit') {
  const markWidth = Math.floor(width / 3);
  const markHeight = Math.floor((sheet.anchorY * sheet.scale * 2) / 3);
  ctx.strokeStyle = theme.warn;
  ctx.lineWidth = theme.borderWidth;
  ctx.strokeRect(-Math.floor(markWidth / 2), -markHeight, markWidth, markHeight);
}
```

A 4px `--tb-warn` **hollow rectangle** around the struck fighter. It is drawn on every hit, which — across the committed corpus — is 146 `attack` submissions plus one `special`, making it the single most repeated visual event in the product.

The comment above it explains, correctly, why it is better than what it replaced: Story 4.1 painted `globalAlpha = 0.55` over the whole 600×600 frame, a red pane across a third of the arena, and the hard bracket fixed that. It was the right fix under the rules of its day. **Two of those rules have since moved and the third never applied.** The 2026-08-07 ruling released `apps/web/src/render/**/*.ts` from the no-translucency and no-`globalAlpha` rules; Story 11.1 recorded the release, and Story 11.2 shipped a whole impact FX sheet the arena can draw with. The bracket has outlived its reason.

And the art for the replacement already ships and is used by nothing:

```
apps/web/public/sprites/martial-hero/take-hit---white-silhouette.png
```

A white silhouette hit-flash frame, committed, provenanced in `docs/ASSETS.md`, drawn nowhere. `apps/web/public/fx/fx_sheet.png` and its `layout.json` also ship, from Story 11.2, and the juice layer already draws sparks from them at the point of contact — so what is missing is not an effects system, it is the **fighter's own** reaction reading as a hit rather than as a box.

## What replaces it

Two things, and the second is the one that matters:

1. **A flash on the fighter, not a frame around them.** The `take-hit---white-silhouette.png` frame composited over the struck fighter for the hit clip's duration — a solid form where the fighter is, which is what a fighting game does and what the bracket was gesturing at from outside. Alpha and additive compositing are both available inside the arena boundary now.
2. **Nothing hollow.** The defect is not the colour, it is the outline. A rectangle with an empty interior is the universal visual language for "this is a debug overlay", and no amount of recolouring changes that reading.

`hero/raster.ts` constrains this as it constrains everything in `render/`: it is a second real `Canvas2D` over a palette-index buffer that throws on an unknown `fillStyle`, and it declares `globalAlpha` without honouring it. The hero must still build, and if the flash cannot be represented there, the hero draws the un-flashed fighter rather than the build failing — that degrade must be deliberate and recorded, not discovered.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** an arena canvas on the frame a hit lands, **When** the gate samples it, **Then** the warn-coloured pixels do not form a hollow rectangle: the check `no-debug-hitbox`, added to `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` by this story, takes the bounding box of contiguous warn-coloured pixels and fails if its interior is empty while its four edges are inked. This is the defect stated as a measurement, and it is what stops a later story quietly reintroducing an outline.

**Given** the same frame, **When** sampled, **Then** the struck fighter carries a visible flash: the check `hit-reads-as-impact` requires the struck fighter's own pixel region to differ from its unstruck frame by more than a threshold the story records. A hit that changes nothing on the fighter is the bracket removed and nothing put in its place.

**Given** the source, **When** `scripts/audit-invariants.sh` runs, **Then** no `strokeRect` with `theme.warn` remains in `apps/web/src/render/`. The pixel check catches the picture; this catches the pattern coming back in a place the gate does not sample.

**Given** `apps/web/public/sprites/martial-hero/take-hit---white-silhouette.png`, **When** this story completes, **Then** it is loaded and drawn, and its row in `docs/ASSETS.md` reflects that it is in use. A shipped-and-unused asset is the Story 9.7 defect in miniature.

**Given** all three surfaces, **When** a hit lands on each, **Then** each shows the new treatment. Spectate got the juice layer in Story 11.6 and must not be left on the old artist path — the Spectate regression survived for an epic by not being mentioned.

**Given** the console, **When** the sheet loads, **Then** `console-clean` passes. The loaders warn rather than throw, so a silhouette that failed to decode reports itself only there and on screen looks like a deliberate choice not to flash.

**Given** a visitor with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, **When** a hit lands, **Then** the flash still draws — it is a state, not a motion — while the shake and the particles stay suppressed exactly as `buildJuiceTrack` already suppresses them.

**Given** the hero raster, **When** it builds, **Then** it builds. Its treatment of the flash is recorded in this story either way.

**Given** any committed Command Log, **When** replayed, **Then** its Final-State Hash is unchanged.

## Visual check finding

Run label `12-8-impact-fx-replaces-the-red-box`, Chrome 1440×900 and 390×844, gate exit 0, `console-clean`,
0 waived. PNGs under `docs/visual/12-8-impact-fx-replaces-the-red-box/`.

- **Replay player (`#app`)** — draws sprites, backdrop and full HUD; captured at
  the film tail (TIME OVER / P2 WINS), fighters centred at roughly a third of
  frame height, which matches the reference `shots/04_local_match.png` framing.
  No hollow warn rectangle anywhere. The flash is a per-hit event and the
  capture did not land on one; the pixel checks below are what prove it draws.
- **Arcade (`#arcade`)** — a live Play-vs-CPU canvas at the opening positions,
  camera-framed (`camera-frames-the-fight` 32% of frame). No debug box.
- **Spectate (`#spectate`)** — `desktop-spectate.png` caught a **live hit**
  (spectate-05, tick 1140): the struck fighter reads as a solid white form with
  the Story 11.2 impact burst at the point of contact, and no red wireframe
  anywhere. This is the story's whole point, photographed.
- **Mobile** — the arena scales to the 390px viewport; `no-horizontal-overflow`
  passes (scrollWidth 390 == clientWidth 390).

**The flash, measured (`hit-reads-as-impact`).** The check reads the largest
contiguous near-white region in the arena band, per frame, across a scrub of
`#app` and a run of `#spectate`. With the flash on, that blob reaches **7201 px
on `#app` and 5893 on `#spectate`**, and is 0 on unstruck frames. To prove the
check is not carried by the pre-existing additive impact sparks — the exact
defect an independent review caught in the first version of it — the flash was
disabled and the gate re-run: the blob then tops out at **314 on `#app` and 9 on
`#spectate`**, and the check **fails**. The `900` threshold sits between the two
with roughly 3× margin below and 6× above, so the check measures the flash and
cannot pass without it. `no-debug-hitbox` reports **0 hollow warn boxes across 37
sampled frames on all three surfaces**.

**Scope of the flash treatment.** `hit-reads-as-impact` is asserted on `#app`
(deterministic scrub) and `#spectate` (autoplay, dense with hits) — the two
surfaces the gate can bring to a landed hit without luck. `#arcade` draws the
identical flash (one shared `artistFor` in `startup.ts` dresses all three
surfaces; `animation.test.ts` pins the composite), but its Match hangs on the
visitor and cannot be driven to a deterministic hit, so it is covered by
`no-debug-hitbox` (which does sweep `#arcade`) and the shared-path unit test
rather than by a staged live hit.

**The geometry compromise.** The silhouette is a Martial Hero pose and the
roster packs it plays over (`clawde`/`chatty`/…) are a different size and anchor.
It is drawn at its **own** geometry (`anchorY 120`, `scale 3`) so it stands a
~fighter-sized white form on the struck fighter's floor, rather than mapped onto
the pack's dest rect where it would float a quarter-height blob at chest level.
It is therefore a foreign outline, not the struck fighter's exact silhouette —
but it reads unambiguously as an impact flash, which is what the story asks for,
and new art is explicitly out of scope. Accepted.

**Reduced motion (AC7).** The flash is drawn by the fighter artist, which
`renderer.ts` calls unconditionally per fighter; the `prefers-reduced-motion`
path lives only in `buildJuiceTrack` (`juice.ts`), which suppresses the shake and
the particles. The artist takes no motion flag, so the flash is a *state* that
draws regardless of the preference while the shake and sparks stay suppressed —
the criterion holds by construction, not by a separate branch.

**Hero raster (AC8).** It builds. `hero/` draws with the block artist, which
issues neither the old `strokeRect` bracket nor the new flash — the sprite
artist's flash `drawImage` is never reached on the raster surface (whose
`drawImage` throws by design). The recorded treatment is therefore the
deliberate degrade: the hero draws the un-flashed block fighter, unchanged from
before this story.

## Independent review

An Opus reviewer read the diff against a scratch worktree at the starting commit
and found the first implementation was **broken in a way every gate missed**.
Its findings and their resolution:

- **F1 (critical) — the flash drew the wrong cell.** `CLIP_FRAME_COUNTS.hit` is
  1, so the frame is always 0, and cell 0 of the strip is a *coloured* take-hit
  frame; only cell 1 is white. The shipped flash was a discoloured ghost.
  **Fixed:** the artist and `loadHitFlash` now draw the white cell by its offset
  (`sx 200`), and a new test decodes the PNG and pins that offset.
- **F2 (high) — geometry mismatch.** The Martial Hero silhouette was mapped onto
  the roster pack's dest rect, floating a quarter-height blob at chest level.
  **Fixed:** it is drawn at its own `anchorY`/`scale`, standing on the floor at
  roughly the fighter's height. Recorded as an accepted compromise above.
- **F3 (high) — the gate check was vacuous.** The reviewer proved a
  count-and-spread check passed on the parent commit with no flash at all,
  carried by the pre-existing additive sparks. **Fixed:** the check now measures
  the largest contiguous near-white *blob*, calibrated against a flash-off run
  (baseline ≤314, signal ≥5893, threshold 900) — see the finding above.
- **F4 (medium) — the composite-restore test was self-fulfilling.** It asserted
  only that *some* draw used `source-over`. **Fixed:** the test now asserts the
  surface's *final* composite mode is restored; deleting the artist's restore
  turns it red.
- **F5 (medium) — a stalled flash decode stranded all artists on the block
  artist.** **Fixed:** the flash is read through a getter at draw time and never
  awaited on any artist's critical path; a slow or failed decode costs only the
  flash.
- **F6 (medium) — AC8 not recorded in the story.** **Fixed:** the finding above
  records the hero degrade and this section records the review.
- **F7 (low-med) — ASSETS.md stated a false fact** ("four white cells").
  **Fixed:** it now says only the second cell is white.
- **F8, F9 (low) — the audit sweep and `no-debug-hitbox` are pattern proxies.**
  **Declined, with reason:** the audit greps a direct `strokeStyle = theme.warn`
  assignment, which is how a warn stroke actually reaches the canvas, and it
  fires on *any* position, so it — not the bbox pixel check — is the robust guard
  against the bracket returning; `no-debug-hitbox` is the pixel confirmation
  beside it. Strengthening either to defeat a contrived multi-statement
  reintroduction is not worth the added surface today.
- **F10 (low) — `hit-reads-as-impact` not asserted on `#arcade`.** **Declined,
  with reason:** the arcade Match cannot be driven to a deterministic hit;
  `#arcade` draws the identical shared-artist flash and is swept by
  `no-debug-hitbox`. Recorded in the finding.
- **F11 (low) — no reduced-motion / all-surface unit test.** **Declined, with
  reason:** both hold by construction (the flash is an artist state with no
  motion flag; all three surfaces are dressed by one `artistFor`), recorded in
  the finding rather than pinned by a test that would assert a structural fact.
- **F12 (low) — flash dims unasserted against the PNG.** **Fixed:** the new
  decode test pins the white cell.
