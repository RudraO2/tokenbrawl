# Story 12.9 — Seven sounds becomes a soundtrack, and the sound is on

As a visitor, I want the fight to sound like a fight from the moment I interact with it, so that the loudest thing about the product is not silence.

## The gap, measured

`apps/web/public/audio/` holds **seven** files:

```
music_battle.mp3  sfx_hit_h.mp3  sfx_hit_l.mp3  sfx_ko.mp3
sfx_special.mp3   vo_ko.mp3      vo_ultimate.mp3
```

`<REF>/game_assets/audio/` holds **81**.

`DEFAULT_AUDIO_TUNING` in `apps/web/src/render/audio.ts` names exactly those seven cues: a looping music bed, a light hit, a heavy hit, a KO, an Ultimate sound, a KO voice line and an Ultimate voice line. There is **no block, no whiff, no footstep, no announcer, no crowd and no UI cue**, and no per-character voice — every fighter is voiced by the same two files.

Sound is also **off by default on the surface a visitor is most likely to encounter**. Story 11.6 measured it and recorded the reason: *"an ambient surface that starts making noise unprompted is a defect, so Spectate's sound is off until asked for."* The markup ships `aria-pressed="false"` and `Sound: off`, and on a fresh load with the stream running the graph records **0 sources started and 0 gain writes** for the whole session.

## Reversing the default, honestly

That Story 11.6 decision was correct for what the page was then: five panels all mounted at once, three of them animating, Spectate looping forever whether or not you were looking. Sound on by default there would have meant a site that starts talking at a visitor who has not chosen anything.

Story 12.4 changed the premise. There is now one screen at a time and a hidden screen is idle. A visitor on the watch screen has chosen to watch. So the default flips — and *how* it flips is the part that must not be sloppy:

- **The control reads ON on first load** and remains a visible, persistent, keyboard-reachable toggle. A visitor who wants silence gets it in one click, on every screen.
- **No sound is produced before a gesture,** because no browser would allow it. Chrome's `AudioContext was not allowed to start` notice is already on `scripts/visual-gate.mjs`'s console allowlist with a comment naming this story: `audio-bus.ts` builds the graph early and resumes it on the first interaction, which is the correct shape. "On by default" therefore means *armed*: the first click, keypress or tap anywhere resumes the context and the fight becomes audible.
- **The choice persists.** A visitor who turns it off does not have it turned back on by a reload.

That is a real reversal of a recorded decision, and it is recorded here as one rather than slipped in.

## What the new cues are

Copied from `<REF>/game_assets/audio/`, which is the author's own prior project and the same owner — `docs/ASSETS.md`'s "The 'author's own prior project' is the NextGen AI Arena reference" section settles that, and `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` says not to re-open it. Copied **bytes** are fine; the reference's absolute path and project name must never travel into `apps/web/public/` in a filename or in file contents, `.md` included — `packages/cli/src/extraction-exclusion.test.ts` enforces it (Story 9.1, AD-16).

The cue set this story is judged on:

| Cue class | Why it is on the list |
|---|---|
| block / guard | `block` is 105 of the 379 submissions in the committed corpus and makes no sound at all |
| whiff | an attack that misses is currently indistinguishable from one that lands, minus the hit |
| footstep on `advance` / `retreat` | 102 submissions, silent |
| round announcer (`ROUND ONE`, `FIGHT`, `K.O.`, `TIME OVER`) | Story 12.7 draws these and they are the reference's loudest moments |
| crowd bed | the stage has a crowd in it (`<REF>/shots/04_local_match.png`); an arena with a silent crowd reads as empty |
| UI cues (move, confirm, back) | the select screen and the router have no sound |
| per-character voice | four fighters sharing one voice is the same defect shape as four packs with two wired |

Every cue goes on one of the **existing three buses** (`music`, `sfx`, `voice`) through `buildAudioTrack`, placed on a **clock frame** — never on a wall clock. The reference's audio runs on `setTimeout`, `performance.now()` and `AudioContext.currentTime`, which is one of the four mechanisms `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` forbids copying; this repo solved it in Story 9.6 and the solution is what every new cue uses. **Copy the sound, never the plumbing.**

**Budget matters.** 81 files is not the target; the target is the cue table above. Every added byte is on the critical path of a static site with no CDN, so the story records the total payload it added and how it is loaded (late, off the critical path, exactly as the sprite packs are).

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a visitor loading the site for the first time, **When** the sound control renders, **Then** it reads ON and `aria-pressed="true"` — the check `sound-on-by-default`, added to `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` by this story.

**Given** that visitor, **When** they click anywhere on the page, **Then** `AudioContext.state` becomes `running` and the first cue plays — the check `audio-starts-on-first-gesture`, which reads the context state and counts started sources after one synthetic click.

**Given** a visitor who turns sound off and reloads, **When** the page comes back, **Then** it is still off, and no source starts. Same check, inverted arm.

**Given** every cue named in `DEFAULT_AUDIO_TUNING`, **When** the gate fetches its URL, **Then** each returns 200 — the check `audio-cues-resolve`. A cue name with no file behind it is silent in exactly the way a missing cue is, and nothing on screen says so.

**Given** a Match in which a fighter blocks, whiffs, walks, is KO'd and fires an Ultimate, **When** it plays with sound enabled, **Then** a cue is emitted for each, on the correct bus, on the frame the event lands. Asserted in the suite against the audio sink, because a bus assignment is not visible in a screenshot.

**Given** two different fighters firing an Ultimate, **When** each plays, **Then** the voice cue differs. Per-character voice is a table keyed by `RosterId` beside `ROSTER_NAMES`, and Story 12.5 is what makes the second key reachable.

**Given** a visitor on a screen that is not showing, **When** it is hidden, **Then** it produces no sound. Story 12.4's `hidden-screens-are-idle` covers the canvas; this story extends it to the audio graph.

**Given** the console, **When** the page runs with sound on, **Then** `console-clean` passes. The only audio-related line tolerated is Chrome's own autoplay-policy notice, which is already allowlisted with a comment naming this story — and that allowlist entry says this story owns making the resume automatic on the first gesture, which is AC2 above.

**Given** every audio file added, **When** `scripts/audit-invariants.sh` and `packages/cli/src/extraction-exclusion.test.ts` run, **Then** neither the reference's path nor its project name appears anywhere under `apps/web/public/`, and every added file has a row in `docs/ASSETS.md` with its source and licence, checked and dated.

**Given** the Final-State Hash of every committed Command Log, **When** verified, **Then** unchanged. Audio is presentation.

## Blocker — awaiting operator

Verified on 2026-08-11, before writing code. **AC5 is unbuildable within this
story's own rules**, and the resolution is a product/asset decision, not an
engineering one — so this parks rather than ships a guessed mechanic or an
out-of-provenance asset.

AC5 requires a cue for a fighter that **blocks, whiffs, and walks** (KO and
Ultimate already have cues). Two independent facts block those three:

1. **No source asset exists.** The story mandates every new cue be *"Copied from
   `<REF>/game_assets/audio/`"* — the only sanctioned source, per INV-8
   (self-hosted only) and `docs/ASSETS.md`'s provenance rule. That directory holds
   exactly **81 files**, enumerated in full during this check. **None is a block,
   whiff, footstep, or crowd sound** (no `step`/`guard`/`block`/`whiff`/`swing`/
   `crowd`/`amb`/`cheer`). There is nothing to copy for three of AC5's five events
   and for the cue table's "crowd bed" row.

2. **No film signal to place them on.** The pure audio layer (`render/audio.ts`)
   is derived one-way from the film and is hash-neutral by construction. `block`
   and `advance`/`retreat` are reflex actions that leave **no committed-action
   trace** in the film — `committedAction ∈ {NONE, ATTACK, SPECIAL, JUMP,
   HITSTUN}` (`packages/env-fighter/src/frames.ts`). Sounding them "on the frame
   the event lands" (AC5) therefore needs new **film-event derivation**, which
   lives only in `render/juice.ts` — a file this story's **Scope** section does
   not list. So even if an asset existed, there is no in-scope place to derive the
   trigger.

The cue table also names **crowd bed** (no source asset, fact 1) which is
likewise unbuildable.

### What *is* achievable, if the cue set is descoped

So the operator can split or retune the story rather than block it wholesale, the
reference **does** contain assets for a large, clean, AC-satisfying subset that
needs neither a new asset source nor a `juice.ts` derivation:

- **AC1/AC2/AC3 — "the sound is on":** sound on by default, armed on first
  gesture, persisted across reload. Fully doable in the listed Scope
  (`spectate/panel.ts` + the shell's sound control + `localStorage` in
  `startup.ts`), and the three named `visual-gate.mjs` checks are all about
  exactly this.
- **AC4 — `audio-cues-resolve`:** doable.
- **AC6 — per-character voice:** `vo_<id>_transform` (Ultimate) and `vo_<id>_ko`
  exist for clawde/chatty/gemini/grokk; a `ROSTER_VOICES` table keyed by
  `RosterId` beside `ROSTER_NAMES` (Scope lists `roster.ts` for exactly this) with
  the caster's `RosterId` threaded through `buildAudioTrack` satisfies it.
- **Round announcer / UI cues (cue-table rows, not ACs):** `vo_round1`, `vo_fight`,
  `sfx_ko`, `vo_choose`, `sfx_bell` all exist.

### What the operator must decide

One of:

1. **Provide the missing audio** (block, whiff, footstep, crowd) into
   `<REF>/game_assets/audio/`, or sanction a new licensed source and its
   `docs/ASSETS.md` provenance, **and** rule that `render/juice.ts` may gain the
   block/whiff/footstep event derivation (position-delta footstep; `committedZone`
   block; attack-active-without-connect whiff); **or**
2. **Descope AC5** to the events that have assets and a film signal (KO, Ultimate,
   and — via new derivation if (1)'s juice.ts ruling is granted — whiff), and drop
   the crowd bed, then re-issue as `ready-for-dev`.

Until then this story cannot reach `done` honestly, and the "sound is on" half was
deliberately **not** half-built around the blocker, per the loop's
don't-stub-around-a-blocker rule.
