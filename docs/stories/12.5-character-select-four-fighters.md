# Story 12.5 — Character select, and the two fighters nobody can reach

As a visitor, I want to choose which fighter I play and who I play against, so that the four characters this project shipped art for are four characters rather than two.

## The gap, measured

`apps/web/src/render/roster.ts` line 49:

```ts
export const DEFAULT_ROSTER: RosterPair = Object.freeze(['clawde', 'chatty'] as const);
```

And the module's own docblock, twenty lines above it:

> *"Character select is still absent (the epic context records it as a known gap), and when it lands it replaces `DEFAULT_ROSTER` at its call sites without touching anything else."*

This story is that landing. What ships today and cannot be selected:

| Shipped | Reachable |
|---|---|
| `apps/web/public/sprites/clawde/` | yes |
| `apps/web/public/sprites/chatty/` | yes |
| `apps/web/public/sprites/gemini/` | **no** |
| `apps/web/public/sprites/grokk/` | **no** |
| `apps/web/public/portraits/{clawde,chatty,gemini,grokk}.png` | **portraits are drawn nowhere at all** |

`ROSTER_IDS` already names all four. `ROSTER_NAMES` already names all four. `ARENA_PALETTE.aura` already has a colour for all four. `spriteLayoutUrlFor` already resolves all four. Everything except the screen exists.

This is the third time this defect has been written down. Story 9.7 is *titled* "four-character custom roster" and shipped four packs while wiring two; Story 10.1's "why this story exists" lists it as one of the three Epic 9 defects that shipped green; Story 11.4 built the roster module around the gap and recorded it. Nothing has been wrong for want of noticing.

## What the screen is

The reference's title screen (`<REF>/shots/03_local_title.png`) is the target: a row of characters, each with a name plate under it, one highlighted. Ours has four rather than eight, and the plates read `CLAWDE`, `CHATTY`, `GEMINI`, `GROKK` — the strings `ROSTER_NAMES` already holds.

**The portraits are the assets to use.** `apps/web/public/portraits/*.png` ship today, are recorded in `docs/ASSETS.md`, and are drawn by nothing. A select screen that invented new art while four portraits sat unused would be the Story 9.7 defect committed a second time.

**Two picks, not one.** The visitor picks their own fighter and their opponent, because an arcade Match is human-versus-bot and both sides are drawn. Spectate's entries are fixed by their committed logs and are not affected; the replay player draws whichever pair its log names.

**`DEFAULT_ROSTER` stays.** It is the pair used when nothing has been chosen — a first load, a direct link to a replay, the hero raster. The selection replaces it at the call sites, exactly as the docblock says.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a visitor on the character-select screen, **When** it renders, **Then** four fighters are offered, each with its portrait from `apps/web/public/portraits/` and its `ROSTER_NAMES` plate, **and** `character-select-reachable` passes with its entry deleted from `docs/visual/known-failures.json`.

**Given** a visitor who selects `GEMINI` and starts a Match, **When** the arena draws, **Then** the fighter on their side is drawn with gemini's sprite pack — the check `selected-fighter-is-drawn`, added to `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` by this story, which plays one Match as gemini and one as grokk and requires the two arena canvases to hash differently while both clear the 2% ink floor. Two selections that produce an identical picture mean the selection reached nothing.

**Given** either previously unreachable fighter, **When** their Match runs, **Then** `console-clean` still passes. The loaders warn rather than throw, so a gemini or grokk pack that fails to decode reports itself only there — and both packs have never been loaded by the running site before this story, so this is the AC most likely to find something.

**Given** a selected fighter, **When** their Ultimate fires, **Then** the cinematic draws **their** aura and **their** name, through `auraFor` and `ROSTER_NAMES`, not clawde's. Story 11.4 built the keying for exactly this and has never had a second key to prove it with.

**Given** a visitor using only the keyboard, **When** they move across the roster and confirm, **Then** the focused fighter carries the visible 3px `--tb-accent` outline and the selection is confirmable without a pointer.

**Given** a visitor on a 390×844 phone, **When** the select screen shows, **Then** `no-horizontal-overflow` still passes. Four portraits in a row is the obvious layout and the obvious way to reintroduce the defect Story 12.4 just cleared.

**Given** any committed Command Log, **When** replayed, **Then** its Final-State Hash is unchanged. Who is drawn is presentation; the simulation has no notion of a character.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/render/roster.ts` (a selection type and its accessors — `DEFAULT_ROSTER` stays as the unselected default), a new character-select screen under `apps/web/src/shell/` registered on the route Story 12.4 reserved, `apps/web/src/arcade/panel.ts` (start the Match with the selected pair), `apps/web/src/startup.ts` (dress the artists from the selection rather than from the constant), `apps/web/src/styles/`, `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` (`selected-fighter-is-drawn`), `docs/visual/known-failures.json` (delete `character-select-reachable`), `docs/ASSETS.md` if a portrait's row needs its usage updated.

Not in scope: new art of any kind. Every asset this story needs is already committed.

## Frozen contracts touched

None. A character is a presentation key; it appears in no Command Log field and must not be added to one. If this story finds itself wanting a `character` on `AgentIdentityV2`, **stop and escalate** — the identity is the Agent, and two Deployments of the same model drawn as different fighters would make the log lie about who fought.

## Invariants at risk

- **AD-14** — Arcade Matches stay excluded from rating by construction (`kind: "human"`). Choosing a fighter changes nothing about eligibility.
- **AD-15** — presentation only.

## Two regimes

The select screen is **page chrome**: flat fills, square corners, 3–4px `--tb-ink` borders, `6px 6px 0` hard shadows, press-displaces on `:active`, stepped `--tb-step` motion. A portrait is an `<img>` in a bordered box, not a glowing card. The arena's release covers `apps/web/src/render/**/*.ts` only, and `roster.ts` is inside it — but this story adds no colour there, because `ARENA_PALETTE` already holds all four auras.

## Test plan

- Every `ROSTER_IDS` entry has a portrait file, a name, an aura and a resolvable layout URL. A fifth id added later fails this test rather than shipping half-wired — which is the whole Story 9.7 lesson made mechanical.
- A selection flows through to the sprite layout URL, the aura and the cinematic's name in one assertion each.
- With no selection made, the pair is `DEFAULT_ROSTER`.
- Keyboard traversal reaches all four and confirms.

**The new check was mutation-tested rather than assumed.** Replacing `onPick` in
`startup.ts`'s `mountSelect` with a no-op — a roster that renders four cards and reaches
nothing — makes `selected-fighter-is-drawn` FAIL with both arenas hashing identically
(`74500624` twice), while `character-select-reachable` stays green. That pair of results
is the whole argument for adding the second check: the first one reads text on a screen,
and text on a screen is exactly what two unreachable fighters already had for three
epics.

**What the check measures, after the review sharpened it.** One Match as gemini and one
as grokk, from a fresh page each time, same seed, both hanging at the first Decision
Point so the fighters stand in identical positions. The arena below the HUD band is
hashed in two halves: the picked side must move and the unpicked side must not. Measured:
`gemini left=611532844 right=-822394502` and `grokk left=1037704431 right=-822394502` —
the right half is byte-identical across the two runs, which is what says the pick reached
the side that made it rather than merely changing the picture. Sprite ink is counted
above `SPRITE_INK_MIN` (1920 and 1715 pixels) rather than through `CANVAS_PROBE`'s
whole-canvas ratio, which reports ~98% on an arena with no fighters drawn at all because
the backdrop fills the frame.

`console-clean` passes on both fighters' Matches. That is the first time gemini's and
grokk's sprite packs have ever been decoded by the running site.

## Independent review — what was fixed, and what was not

An independent review of the diff raised eleven findings. Two were HIGH and both were
real; nine were fixed or recorded.

**Fixed — the visitor's sprite packs were reaching Spectate (HIGH).** `dressArtist`
pushed every decoded pack to `panels.spectate`, and `loadRosterArt` called it with the
*chosen* pair. Pick GROKK and go to `#/watch` and the stream's left fighter was drawn
with grokk's sprites over a committed log recorded as somebody else — with the cinematic
underneath still drawing clawde's aura and the plate `CLAWDE`, because `spectate/panel.ts`
correctly passes `DEFAULT_ROSTER`. **The defect was already committed in this story's own
visual record**: the first run's `desktop-spectate.png` shows grokk where 12.4's shows
clawde, and no check looked. Spectate now has its own `streamDressing`, loaded once from
`DEFAULT_ROSTER`.

**Fixed — the shared Ultimate sheet took two fighters' cut-ins away from the stream
(HIGH).** `imageUrlsFor(layout, pair)` fetches only the pair's portraits, and that sheet
was handed to Spectate. Choosing gemini+grokk left `portraitFor('clawde')` undefined, so
every committed log featuring clawde or chatty silently dropped to Story 10.4's
banner-and-band — no warning, no failing check. Spectate now gets its own sheet for
`DEFAULT_ROSTER`. Both loads are memoised per fighter and per pair, so the first load,
where the two pairs are the same pair, costs one fetch rather than two.

**Fixed — the check could not tell a correct pick from a swapped one, and its ink floor
could not fail.** Both described above.

**Fixed — nothing photographed the screen the story is about.** `#select` is a fourth
entry in the gate's `SURFACES` now. It holds no canvas, so it is not a rendering surface;
it is a screen a visitor spends time on, and a portrait that decoded and laid out at zero
height would have passed every check in the file.

**Fixed — the gate left its own archive on a chosen fighter.** The roster experiment runs
last in each viewport pass, after the captures.

**Fixed — the mobile plate overflow, the stale comments in `shell/screens.ts`, and a test
fake that manufactured a node for any selector** (so a typo'd `data-select-pick` would
have passed every case in `select.test.ts`). The fake now answers `null` for anything the
markup does not declare, which also made the mount's own failure path drivable.

**Declined — the replay player follows the selection, and the story's line about the
player is about logs that name a pair.** Story text: *"the replay player draws whichever
pair its log names."* No committed log names one — a character is presentation and appears
in no Command Log field, which this story is forbidden from changing — so the player's
answer has to come from somewhere else, and `DEFAULT_ROSTER` was only ever a stand-in for
it. The player is also where an arcade Match's replay lands, and a visitor who just fought
as GROKK being shown their own fight back as clawde would be the worse of the two wrongs.
So the player follows the selection, and the cost is stated plainly: browsing the roster
changes who the *demo* replay is drawn as. **Deferred: a Command Log that names the
characters its Match was drawn with**, owed by `12-11`, which regenerates the corpus and is
the first story with a reason to record one.

**Recorded — the desktop select screen is not full-bleed and has no stage behind it.**
Same shape as 12.4's deferral for the match screen, same owner: `12-6`.
