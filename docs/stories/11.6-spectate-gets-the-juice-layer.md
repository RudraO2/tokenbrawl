# Story 11.6 — The stream everyone watches gets the effects

As a visitor, I want the always-running AI-vs-AI stream to have the same impact
effects and sound as the replay player, so that the surface I look at first and
longest is not the flattest thing on the page.

## Reference material

**Read `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` first** for the reference project's location and
the four mechanisms that may never be copied.

This story has **no reference counterpart** — the reference has no ambient
spectate stream. Everything here is wiring this repo's own existing juice and
audio layers into a surface that never got them, so nothing is copied and
nothing new is designed. If this story starts inventing a look, it has gone
wrong: it should be reusing `drawJuicedFrame` and `buildAudioTrack` verbatim.

## The gap, measured

`spectate/panel.ts` calls `drawFrame` **directly** — never `drawJuicedFrame` —
and `spectate/walk.ts` builds its clock from `film.frames.length` rather than
from a juice track. No audio track is built at all.

So Spectate has, and has always had: **no hitstop, no sparks, no damage numbers,
no cinematic, no sound.** Story 10.4's visual check confirmed it by watching 541
rendered frames covering the whole of `spectate-03` — the one streamed Match
that contains an Ultimate — and counting **zero** banner frames and **zero**
accent pixels below the HUD.

This is a Story 9.5-era gap that has been logged three times (10.4 observation
#1, 10.5 observation #1, 10.6) and fixed never. It is the largest single
presentation deficit in the project, because Spectate is the ambient surface: an
Ultimate on the replay player fires once when a visitor presses a button, and
Spectate runs forever.

## The thing that makes this non-trivial

Wiring the juice layer **lengthens every Spectate Match's clock**. A juice track
is the film's length plus every hitstop hold plus every cinematic freeze —
`spectate-03` is 480 film frames and 630 clock frames.

`manifest.json` records a `frameCount` per entry, and
`spectate/manifest.ts`'s `offsetForNow` uses it to compute where a visitor
arriving right now should join the loop. **That recorded number is currently the
film's length, not the track's.** Left alone, every entry's join offset drifts by
up to a third of a Match and the loop's timing goes wrong.

This is the real work of the story and it must be solved deliberately, not
discovered.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** the Spectate stream, **When** a hit lands, **Then** it draws the same
impact art, hitstop and damage numbers the replay player draws, through the
**existing** juice layer — extended, never reimplemented alongside it.

**Given** an Ultimate in a streamed Match, **When** it plays, **Then** the
cinematic plays there too.

**Given** the juice layer lengthening the clock, **When** `manifest.json` is
regenerated, **Then** each entry's `frameCount` is the **track's** length, and
`offsetForNow` lands a visitor at the same point in the loop it always did.
Regeneration is by the committed script, not by hand.

**Given** the stream, **When** it plays, **Then** its audio follows the page's
existing three-bus layer and its mute/unlock behaviour is unsurprising — an
ambient surface that starts making noise unprompted is a defect, so the default
must be deliberate and recorded in the story.

**Given** a visitor's browser with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, **When**
Spectate draws, **Then** it honours the preference exactly as the player does.

**Given** any streamed Match, **When** its Final-State Hash is compared before
and after, **Then** it is unchanged (AD-15), and every committed
`spectate-0N.command-log.json` still verifies.

## Frozen contracts touched

None. `manifest.json` is this project's own artefact, not a frozen contract —
but check that before changing its shape.

## Invariants at risk

- **AD-15** — presentation only; the Command Logs are not regenerated, only the
  manifest's derived frame counts.
- **AD-17** — the default stream stays a manifest walk over already-committed
  logs. Nothing here may turn it into a computation triggered by a visitor.
- **INV-3** — `loopStartEpochMs` is a fixed anchor constant, not a timestamp
  taken at generation time. It must stay one.

## Test plan

- Spectate's call sequence contains the juice layer's calls where the replay
  player's does.
- A streamed Match containing an Ultimate draws the cinematic.
- `offsetForNow` lands at the same *proportion* through a Match before and after
  the frame-count change.
- Every committed Command Log still verifies its hash.
- Reduced motion produces a still stream.

## Exit gate

`npm test` green **AND** `bash scripts/audit-invariants.sh` exiting zero **AND**
Story 10.1's visual gate — with Spectate captured mid-hit **and** mid-Ultimate,
which is precisely the capture that has come back empty every time it has been
taken so far. The finding is recorded here.
