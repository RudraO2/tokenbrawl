# Story 12.3 — The arena gets a camera, and stops drawing fighters off the edge

As a viewer, I want the frame to follow the fight and keep both fighters inside it, so that the action is in the middle of the picture instead of pressed against one wall with four fifths of the screen empty.

## The gap, measured

`apps/web/src/render/backdrop.ts`, line 9, in the module's own docblock:

> *"Static, not parallaxed: the arena is a single fixed horizontal axis with **no camera**, so there is nothing for parallax to be relative to."*

That is accurate and it is the defect. `DEFAULT_FIGHTER_CONFIG` runs the arena from `arenaMin: 0` to `arenaMax: 960`, and `main.ts` sets `CANVAS_WIDTH = 960`. Simulation units map **1:1** onto device pixels with no transform anywhere between them, so:

- A fighter at `x = 960` — the legal right wall — is drawn **centred on x = 960**, which is the canvas's right edge. `artist.ts` draws from `-width / 2`, so half the sprite renders outside the frame. The same happens at `x = 0`. **Both walls clip the fighter in half.**
- `startPosition` is `[320, 640]`, so a Match opens with the fighters occupying the middle third and the outer thirds empty. Story 12.1's gate run recorded exactly this on `#app`: *"Fighters sit far right with roughly four fifths of the frame empty."*
- The reference (`<REF>/shots/04_local_match.png`) frames both fighters centre-screen at roughly a third of frame height, with the stage running past them on both sides. That framing is a camera doing work, not a wider canvas.

Both halves — the follow and the clamp — belong in this one story. A camera without a clamp moves the clipping bug rather than fixing it, and a clamp without a camera is a fixed frame that happens to be inset.

## What the camera is, and what it must not be

A **pure function of the frame**, exactly like everything else in `render/`:

```
cameraFor(fighterA, fighterB, viewport, config) -> { x, scale }
```

No accumulator, no easing across calls, no `performance.now()`. The reference's own camera smooths through a mutable module-level `state` — one of the four mechanisms `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` forbids copying, and `source-discipline.test.ts` bans it outright. **Copy the picture, never the plumbing.** If smoothing is wanted, it is an ease over `frame.index` or over the pair's separation, computed fresh every call, so a scrub to frame 90 draws the same camera as a play-through that reaches frame 90 (INV-3, Story 11.3's `pulseLevel` is the pattern to follow).

Three behaviours, in priority order:

1. **Follow** — the camera centres on the midpoint between the two fighters.
2. **Clamp** — the camera never shows past `arenaMin` or `arenaMax`, and a fighter standing on either wall is drawn **whole**, inside the frame, with their full sprite width visible.
3. **Zoom** — the scale widens as the fighters separate and tightens as they close, bounded at both ends, so a corner exchange reads at the size the reference reads at.

`hero/raster.ts` is a second real implementation of `Canvas2D` and it resolves `fillStyle` through a `Map` that throws on a miss; it also has no `window`. Whatever transform the camera applies must go through calls that surface already has — `translate`, `scale` and `save`/`restore` are all used by `artist.ts` today. A camera that only works in the browser silently desynchronises the README hero from the player.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a visitor watching any of the three surfaces at the start of a Match, **When** the frame is drawn, **Then** both fighters' sprites lie wholly inside the canvas and neither is cropped by an edge — the check `fighters-inside-frame`, added to `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` by this story, which samples the outermost 4px columns of each arena canvas above the floor line and fails on sprite ink there.

**Given** an arcade Match, **When** the visitor holds `ArrowRight` until their fighter reaches the wall (thirty presses is past `arenaMax` from `startPosition`), **Then** the fighter is still drawn whole with no ink in the outer columns, and the camera has stopped rather than continuing past the arena — the same check, re-sampled at the wall.

**Given** a Match at its opening positions, **When** the frame is drawn, **Then** the fight is not pressed into one third of the picture: the check `camera-frames-the-fight` requires sprite ink in the middle three fifths of the canvas and none in the outer fifth on either side.

**Given** the replay player scrubbed to an arbitrary frame, **When** the same frame is reached by playing forward instead, **Then** the camera draws identically. Asserted in the suite as a pure-function property, and visible as the gate's two captures agreeing.

**Given** the hero raster, **When** `apps/web/scripts/build-hero.mts` runs, **Then** it builds without a palette miss and the hero image shows the same framing the browser shows. A camera the hero cannot draw is a camera that has forked the two surfaces.

**Given** any committed Command Log, **When** it is replayed, **Then** its Final-State Hash is unchanged. This story draws differently; it simulates nothing.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/render/renderer.ts` (the camera and the transform), a new `apps/web/src/render/camera.ts` if the arithmetic wants its own home, `apps/web/src/render/backdrop.ts` (its docblock's "no camera" claim stops being true and must stop being written), `apps/web/src/render/juice-draw.ts` (screen-space effects must land where the camera puts the fighters), `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` (two new checks).

Not in scope: parallax (Story 12.10 — the camera is its prerequisite, which is why that story depends on this one), the HUD's layout (Story 12.6 — the HUD is screen-space and must sit **outside** the camera transform).

## Frozen contracts touched

None. `arenaMin`/`arenaMax` in `packages/env-fighter/src/config.ts` are **not** touched — changing them changes every Final-State Hash in the repository. The camera is a view over the same coordinates.

## Invariants at risk

- **INV-1 / INV-3** — the camera reads `frame.index` and the fighter pair, never a clock. A camera that eases over elapsed time leaks how long a Decision Point took.
- **AD-15** — presentation only.

## Two regimes

Entirely inside `apps/web/src/render/**/*.ts`, so the arena's three released rules apply: translucency, gradients and `globalAlpha` are available. Geometry and type stay global — no third font, no remote asset. Read the "Two regimes" section of `docs/DESIGN.md` before drawing anything.

## Test plan

- `cameraFor` is pure: same arguments, same result, called a thousand times.
- At `arenaMin` and at `arenaMax` the returned frame contains the fighter's full sprite width.
- Scale is bounded at both ends for every separation from `minSeparation` to the arena's full width.
- The HUD's screen-space coordinates are unchanged by any camera value.
- The hero raster builds and its palette is complete.

## Exit gate

`npm test` green **AND** `bash scripts/audit-invariants.sh` exiting zero **AND** `node scripts/visual-gate.mjs` exiting zero with `fighters-inside-frame` and `camera-frames-the-fight` both passing on all three surfaces.

## Independent review — what was fixed, and what was not

An independent review of the diff raised ten findings. Seven were fixed; three are recorded here.

**Fixed.**

- *The compositor's camera had no test at all.* Replacing `applyCamera` in `drawJuicedFrame` with a
  no-op left all 1157 tests green, and the gate could not see it either — it never fires an Ultimate
  and never samples a spark frame. Every spark, damage number, impact cell and Ultimate beam would have
  drawn at the pre-camera position with the call sequence unchanged. Closed by three cases in
  `juice-draw.test.ts` ("the juice is composited in the camera the fighters were drawn in"), which the
  same mutation now fails two of.
- *`camera-frames-the-fight` could not fail.* "Ink in the middle three fifths, none in the outer fifth"
  is satisfied by the pre-camera build at the opening positions, where the pair spans 216..744 of 960.
  It now also bounds the pair's span at 35% of the canvas — a number measured off the reference (~33%)
  and confirmed against both builds (32.0% with the camera, 42.6% without) — and requires at least 500
  ink pixels so one stray pixel cannot satisfy it.
- *The framing check ran on one surface.* Added `fight-is-centred-<viewport>`, which sweeps every
  visible arena canvas on the page and requires the pair's ink centre to lie in the middle third. It is
  the only half of the framing claim that can be asked of `#app` and `#spectate` — both autoplay and
  are never caught at a Match's opening positions — and it is precisely the property Story 12.1
  recorded as violated ("fighters sit far right"). It fails on the pre-camera build at 919 of 960.
- *`fighters-inside-frame` passed on a canvas with no fighter.* `every(c => c.edgeInk === 0)` is true of
  an empty arena, and `canvas-not-blank` reports 98% ink from the screen-space backdrop regardless, so
  a NaN camera scale would have produced two green checks over an arena with nothing in it. Every
  framing check now requires sprite ink to be present.
- *The "pins the floor" case was a tautology.* `(groundY - groundY) * scale + groundY === groundY` is
  true for every scale and never called `applyCamera`. Replaced with a surface that applies the
  transform, plus a case tying `screenXOf` — the model the clamp and zoom cases are written against —
  to the real function, so the suite no longer validates its own model.
- *`renderer.test.ts` promised camera cases that did not exist.* The recorders were added with a
  comment referring to assertions nobody wrote; the same mutation showed that deleting `applyCamera`
  from `drawFrame` failed only the hero GIF's byte diff, whose message reads "expected 212902 to be
  215489". Four real cases now pin the transform, the screen-space/world-space split, and the balance.
- *The juice layer's clamps silently changed meaning.* `0..viewport.width` used to be the frame's
  edges and is now the arena. Left as-is on purpose — it is the better bound — and said so in
  `paintSparks`'s docblock rather than leaving the constants lying about what they do.

**Fixed, and not from the review: the gate flaked once and the cause was real.** On its first strict run
`fighters-inside-frame-desktop` failed with `spectate edge=27@255 span=403..959`, and the next run
passed. The cause is `drawCinematicPlate`: the caster's portrait slides in from
`viewport.width + portraitWidth` and is *supposed* to cross the frame's right edge, because the plate
half of the Ultimate is screen-space framing rather than stage. Spectate autoplays a randomly chosen
Match and one of them threw an Ultimate as the probe fired. A canvas whose frame is letterboxed is now
excluded from the framing checks and the exclusion is reported, never silent, and a check whose every
canvas was excluded fails rather than passing on an empty set. Three consecutive green runs since.

**Declined — the clamp shows stage past `arenaMin`/`arenaMax`, which one reading of AC2 forbids.** The
two halves of that criterion cannot both be met: *"a fighter standing on either wall is drawn whole,
inside the frame, with their full sprite width visible"* requires showing at least half a sprite (104
world px) beyond the wall, and *"the camera never shows past `arenaMin` or `arenaMax`"* forbids showing
any. The implementation takes the first as the operative half — it is the one with a test and a gate
check — and reads the second as *the camera stops rather than continuing to chase a midpoint past the
stage*, which `camera.test.ts`'s "stops rather than chasing a midpoint past the stage" pins. The margin
is half the arena rather than the 104px minimum because `STAGE_MARGIN_RATIO == HEADROOM_RATIO` is what
makes the clamp total (the view is never wider than the stage while both fighters are inside the arena)
*and* what puts the opening frame at the reference's own framing. Nothing draws a wall, so the margin
reads as more floor — which is what the reference frame shows either side of its own pair. **This is a
declared reading, not an oversight, and Story 12.10 should revisit it:** parallax makes the margin
visible for the first time, because a backdrop scrolling against the camera can betray where the stage
ends in a way a screen-space backdrop cannot.

**Declined — reduced motion now gets a camera that pans and zooms.** `prefers-reduced-motion` is
honoured for the shake, the particles and the gauge pulse, and the camera ignores it. That is
deliberate: the camera is framing, not decoration — it is a continuous function of positions the viewer
is already watching move, and it never moves when the fight does not. Locking it would not produce a
still picture; it would produce a fixed frame that cannot draw a wall fighter whole at any scale that
also frames the opening, i.e. it would reinstate the defect this story exists to fix for exactly the
viewers least able to tolerate it. **Deferred: what reduced motion means for camera-driven motion**,
owed by `12-10`, which is the story that adds the first motion genuinely relative to this camera and is
the right place to decide the policy for both at once. `spectate/panel.test.ts`'s reduced-motion case
was narrowed from "no non-zero translate anywhere" to "no non-zero shake translate" to make this
visible rather than to hide it; the shake is still pinned exactly as it was.

**Not a finding, but recorded: `apps/web` now sets `testTimeout: 30_000`.** Four film-walking cases
started timing out against Vitest's 5s default under the parallel load this story's new test files
added — `hero-artefact`'s GIF rebuild, two in `cinematic-neutrality`, one in `juice-neutrality` — with
*which* four varying run to run. Each finishes in under two seconds alone. Commit `76e953f` raised one
such case rather than the project and argued against a blanket timeout; that was right at one case and
is not right at five, because the set is unstable and a suite that is green most of the time teaches
the next session to shrug at a red one. The reasoning is written into `apps/web/vite.config.ts`.
