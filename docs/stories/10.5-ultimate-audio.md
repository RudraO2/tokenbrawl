# Story 10.5 — The Ultimate sounds like one

As a visitor, I want the Ultimate to have its own audio cue, so that it reads even when I am not looking directly at it.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** the three-bus audio layer from Story 9.6, **When** the Ultimate fires, **Then** it plays a distinct cue on the appropriate bus — never a louder reuse of the existing `sfx_hit_h` sample. An Ultimate that sounds like a heavy hit teaches a listener nothing.

**Given** the cue, **When** it is triggered, **Then** the trigger is the same frame-indexed event the cinematic uses (Story 10.4), so audio and picture cannot drift apart on a seek.

**Given** a browser with audio blocked, WebAudio absent, or the context never unlocked by a gesture, **When** the Ultimate fires, **Then** playback is silent and otherwise identical — the fail-soft rule `docs/ASSETS.md` already documents and `createAudioBus` already implements by returning `null`.

**Given** a visitor seeking the timeline across an Ultimate repeatedly, **When** they do, **Then** the cue does not stack or retrigger into a pile of overlapping sources.

**Given** any new audio file, **When** it lands, **Then** `docs/ASSETS.md` records its provenance and licence **in the same change** — never a later, separate commit. Existing audio in this repo is the author's own IP from the NextGen reference project and is recorded that way; a new cue follows the same rule.

**Given** any Match, **When** its Final-State Hash is compared before and after this story, **Then** it is unchanged (AD-15). Audio is never in the simulation path.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/render/audio-bus.ts`, `apps/web/public/audio/`, `docs/ASSETS.md`.

## Frozen contracts touched

None.

## Invariants at risk

- **AD-15** — audio is presentation and must be hash-neutral.
- **INV-8** — the cue is a same-origin static asset. No third-party host, and the site must still render and sound identical offline.

## Test plan

- The Ultimate triggers a cue distinct from the hit SFX.
- A null sink (no WebAudio) plays nothing and throws nothing.
- Repeated seeks across the Ultimate do not stack sources.
- Final-State Hash unchanged.

## Exit gate

`npm test` green **AND** `bash scripts/audit-invariants.sh` exiting zero **AND** `docs/ASSETS.md` updated in the same change **AND** Story 10.1's visual gate (the cinematic and cue verified together on the page, not only in tests).
