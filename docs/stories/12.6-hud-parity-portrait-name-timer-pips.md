# Story 12.6 — The HUD gets the five things it does not have, and stops overlapping itself

As a viewer, I want the HUD to tell me who is fighting, how long is left and who is winning the set, so that the top of the screen is an arcade HUD rather than two bars and a word printed over one of them.

## The gap, measured

Two defects, both recorded by Story 12.1's gate run on 2026-08-10 against the `#spectate` capture:

- **`ULTIMATE READY` draws on top of the gold gauge and is unreadable.** `renderer.ts` places the armed callout at the gauge's own coordinates. `arcadeText` draws a hard shadow in `hudPlate` and a fill in the callout colour, which separates type from a dark ground — and the armed gauge at pulse level 2 is flat arcade gold, which is the one background that treatment cannot survive.
- **`HP 69` overlaps the bar below it.** The numeric readout and the bar occupy the same rows.

And five elements the reference has that this HUD has none of. From `<REF>/shots/04_local_match.png`, left to right: a **portrait** in a bordered plate, the fighter's **name** with a model line under it, the health bar with its damage-lag ghost, the super meter beneath it, a **round timer** in a circle at centre screen, and **round win pips** — small diamonds — flanking the timer. Ours has the bar, the ghost and the meter. It has no portrait, no name, no model line, no timer and no pips.

The name and portrait have had nowhere to come from until now, which is why this story sits after 12.5: `ROSTER_NAMES` and `apps/web/public/portraits/` are the sources, and a selection is what makes them per-fighter rather than a constant.

## What this story is allowed to draw with

`apps/web/src/render/hud.ts` is the drawing vocabulary and it is deliberately narrow: rows of `fillRect`, `drawArcadeBar`, `drawArcadeSegments` and `arcadeText`. The narrowness is load-bearing and its docblock says why — `hero/raster.ts` is a second real implementation of `Canvas2D` over a palette-index buffer whose `fillStyle` map **throws on a miss**, and it has no `clip`, no `Path2D` and no `CanvasGradient`. A HUD element drawn with a gradient object works in the browser and hard-fails the hero build.

So:

- **The portrait is a `drawImage`,** which both surfaces have, from the same `apps/web/public/portraits/<id>.png` the select screen shows. The plate around it is `fillRect` rows in the existing `hudFrame`/`hudPlate`/`hudBevel` colours.
- **Every new colour is an `ARENA_PALETTE` entry or is computed from two of them** by the existing integer channel lerp, and every new colour must be reachable from `ARCADE_HUD_COLOURS` or the hero build fails loudly — which is the design working, not an obstacle.
- **Any text drawn on the canvas answers to the 4.5:1 contrast floor** and its measured ratio is recorded in this story. `docs/DESIGN.md`'s "Two regimes" is explicit: the arena palette is exempt from the text floor for fills, gradients and glows, and *text a visitor reads is not*. The `ULTIMATE READY`-on-gold defect is precisely a contrast failure that was never measured.
- **The HUD is screen-space.** It is drawn outside Story 12.3's camera transform and its coordinates do not move when the camera does.

## The timer, and why it is not a clock

The reference's `89` counts down in seconds. **We do not have seconds and must not acquire them** (INV-1, INV-3). What we have is `frame`'s tick against `DEFAULT_FIGHTER_CONFIG.maxTicks` (1200), which is a pure function of the frame index. The timer displays `maxTicks - tick` mapped onto a two-digit count, integer arithmetic, no division by a frame rate. A scrub to any frame shows the same number as playing to it. Nothing about the display may vary with how long an Agent thought — that is the invariant this whole project is built to protect.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** any of the three surfaces drawing a Match, **When** the HUD is drawn, **Then** the top band carries, per side: a portrait, the fighter's name, the health bar with its ghost, and the super meter; and at centre: the round timer and the round pips — the check `hud-has-all-five`, added to `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` by this story, which samples ink in six named regions of the HUD band and fails naming any region that is empty.

**Given** the armed super gauge, **When** the callout draws, **Then** it is readable: the check `hud-no-overlap` requires the `ULTIMATE READY` callout's rows and the gauge's rows to be disjoint, and this story records the callout colour's measured contrast ratio against whatever it is now drawn on.

**Given** the health readout, **When** it draws, **Then** its rows and the bar's rows are disjoint. Same check.

**Given** a Match at tick 0 and the same Match at tick 600, **When** the HUD is drawn, **Then** the timer reads two different values, and scrubbing back to tick 0 restores the first — the check `hud-timer-counts-down`, which reads the timer region's hash at two scrub positions on `#app`.

**Given** the round pips, **When** no round has been won, **Then** they draw as empty markers rather than not drawing. A pip that appears only once it is earned is a HUD element that moves under the viewer.

**Given** the hero raster, **When** `apps/web/scripts/build-hero.mts` runs, **Then** it builds with a complete palette and the README image shows the same HUD the browser shows.

**Given** a visitor with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, **When** the HUD draws, **Then** it keeps its layout and its information and only stops animating — `pulseLevel` already pins to level 0 and any new animated element follows the same rule.

**Given** any committed Command Log, **When** replayed, **Then** its Final-State Hash is unchanged.

## Invariants at risk

- **INV-1 / INV-3** — the timer is a function of the tick and of nothing else. No `Date`, no `performance.now()`, no frame rate in the arithmetic.
- **AD-15** — presentation only.

## Two regimes

`apps/web/src/render/**/*.ts`, so the arena's three released rules apply — but the two that are **not** released bind hard here: geometry and type stay global (no third font; `THEME.arcadeFont` is the display face at 16px and stays that), and **canvas text answers to the 4.5:1 floor**. Read the "Two regimes" section, including the paragraph on which ratios `render/arena-palette.ts` records and why.

### The contrast ratios, measured

Every ratio below is against `ARENA_PALETTE.hudPlate`, and that is the point of
the `drawHudGround` fill this story adds. Standard WCAG 2.x relative luminance.

| Text | Ground | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| `ULTIMATE READY` — `ARENA_PALETTE.gold` | `hudPlate` | **13.73:1** |
| fighter name — `--tb-ink` | `hudPlate` | **17.87:1** |
| round timer digits — `ARENA_PALETTE.gold` | `hudPlate` | **13.73:1** |
| `TICK …` — `--tb-ink` | `hudPlate` | **17.87:1** |
| `HP … MTR …` — `--tb-ink` | `hudPlate` | **17.87:1** |

All clear the 4.5:1 floor `docs/DESIGN.md`'s "Two regimes" keeps for canvas text
even where the arena's *fills* are released from it.

**The first version of this story recorded those ratios against `--tb-bg`, and
an independent review found that the visitor never sees `--tb-bg` behind a HUD
string.** `options.backdrop?.draw` fills the whole frame before the HUD is drawn,
so every callout, name and readout was being set on the dusk mountains. Measured
against the lit cliff behind the left column, `--tb-muted` — which `TICK` had
carried since Story 4.1 — came out at **1.30:1**, and it is genuinely hard to
read in the pre-fix captures. Two changes followed, and both are the fix rather
than a re-measurement: the type columns and the centre band get a flat `hudPlate`
ground, and `TICK` moved to `--tb-ink` because `--tb-muted` measures **3.81:1**
on that plate and misses the floor. `--tb-muted`'s own recorded 5.42:1 is against
`--tb-bg`, which is not the ground it was ever on here.

### What the new checks measure, after the review sharpened them

`hud-has-all-five` **now runs on all three arena surfaces**, not on the replay
player alone. The review's argument is this repository's own history: a HUD
element wired into one surface and forgotten in another is the defect 12.5 found
in Spectate one story ago, and a check reading `#app` could not see it.

It also counts the element's **content**, not just its plate. The first version
thresholded on `hudFrame` pixels only, and the reported `p1-portrait:336` is
exactly `44² − 40²` — the frame ring, and not one pixel of anybody's face. A
surface that lost its portrait sheet would still draw an aura-filled framed box
and still pass. A portrait region now needs 8 distinct colours (a flat plate has
two; a decoded portrait reports ~1450) and the timer needs 20 gold pixels for its
digits.

**Mutation-tested rather than assumed.** With `drawPortraitPlate`'s `drawImage`
short-circuited — plates still drawn, faces gone — the gate FAILS naming
`/replay:p1-portrait, /replay:p2-portrait, /play:p1-portrait, /play:p2-portrait,
/watch:p1-portrait, /watch:p2-portrait`. Restored, it reports portrait colours
`1457/1490` on the player and arcade and `1406/1442` on Spectate, and timer gold
124/78/132.

`hud-no-overlap` measures **both** text bands now: zero bar-frame pixels in the
callout's rows and zero in the readout's. The review was right that its first
version's callout half was a table restating itself — the probe sampled scrub 0,
where no gauge is armed and the callout is not drawn at all. Bar ink is the
reading that works either way round: a callout band overlapping the gauge finds
the gauge's own outline inside it whether or not the word is on screen. The row
spans are still compared against each other as well, and `renderer.test.ts` ties
that table to the constants the drawing actually uses, so moving the callout back
onto the gauge propagates into the gate rather than being invisible to it.

`ARENA_TOP_PX` moved 130 → 152 because the band grew to 144. It carried none of
the framing checks' evidence with it: `camera-frames-the-fight` reports
`middle=3280 outerFifth=0 span=322..629` and `selected-fighter-is-drawn` reports
`spriteInk=1920/1715`, both byte-identical to 12.5's run. The review noted the
constant was the one hand-copy nothing pinned; `renderer.test.ts` now asserts the
gate's `ARENA_TOP_PX` is at or below no part of `HUD_BOTTOM`, so a story that
grows the band past it fails the suite instead of quietly moving every framing
check onto HUD pixels that advance on their own.

`canvas-not-blank` fell from ~98% to ~80% ink on all three surfaces. That is the
`hudPlate` ground: 36% of the canvas is now a near-black plate under the HUD,
which reads as *not ink*. Well clear of the 2% floor.

## Independent review — what was fixed, and what was not

Nine findings. Six were fixed, three are declined with reasons.

**Fixed — the recorded ratios described a ground the visitor never sees (HIGH).**
Above. This is the finding that changed the product rather than the paperwork:
`TICK` was at 1.30:1 over the backdrop and is now at 17.87:1 over a plate.

**Fixed — `hud-has-all-five` could not fail for a missing portrait or missing
timer digits, and sampled one surface of three (HIGH).** Above, with the mutation
run.

**Fixed — `hud-no-overlap`'s callout half restated its own table.** Above.

**Fixed — `ARENA_TOP_PX` was unpinned.** Above.

**Fixed — the story file was empty.** The exit gate asks for the ratios in this
section and two `renderer.ts` docblocks point a reader at it. They now point at
something.

**Fixed — `drawArcadePlate` overhung its own box below 2px.** `hud.ts`: on a box
one pixel tall the bottom edge landed a row *above* the rectangle, which is the
exact overhang the four-`fillRect` construction is documented as preventing.
Unreachable at 44, 12 and 60, and fixed anyway: a rule with one size it does not
hold for is not a rule.

**Declined — `BANK n` is drawn inside the Token Bank bar's own rows.** True, and
measured: `--tb-ink` on `bank.from` is **1.91:1** and on `bank.to` **4.63:1**, so
the label is genuinely hard to read over the filled part of the meter. It is not
this story's to fix, for two reasons rather than one. It is the *deliberate*
inverted-label pattern Story 11.3 established one branch away in the same
function — `REFLEX  BANK 0` in ground ink on a warn fill — and this story's
acceptance criterion names the *health* readout (`HP … MTR …`) specifically.
More to the point, the obvious fix does not work: the bar is filled from one edge
and mirrored per side, so p1's label sits on violet and p2's sits on the empty
plate behind it, and no single colour clears the floor on both. It needs the
label to get rows of its own, which means growing a band this story already grew
by 26px. **Deferred: a Token Bank readout with a ground of its own**, and no
remaining Epic 12 story touches the Bank — it belongs to `epic-12-retrospective`
to place.

**Declined — the HUD is illegible at 390px.** Measured: the 960-wide backbuffer
renders at about 340 CSS px, a scale of 0.35, so the 16px names land near 5.7px
and the 14px readout near 5px. This is real and it is visible in
`mobile-arcade.png`. It is also pre-existing — `HP … MTR …` and `TICK` have had
it since Story 4.1 — and this story makes it worse by adding three more strings
at that scale. **No waiver is recorded**, because no gate check measures it and a
waiver against no check is a note in the wrong file: every pixel check here reads
the backbuffer, which is viewport-independent, so a check that could see this
would have to measure rendered CSS size rather than pixels. **Deferred: a HUD
that reflows or scales its type below the arena's own width.** The natural owner
is `12-10-stages-and-parallax-depth`, which is the next story to touch how the
arena is presented; if 12.10 declines it, it belongs to the retrospective.

**Declined — AC5's reduced-motion criterion has no new test.** Correct, and it is
vacuous by construction: nothing this story adds animates. The portrait, the
name, the timer and the pips are pure in the frame, and `pulseLevel` — the only
animated thing in the band — is unchanged and still covered by 11.3's cases. A
test asserting that a static element is static would pass on a deleted element.
