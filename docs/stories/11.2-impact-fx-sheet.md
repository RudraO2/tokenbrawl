# Story 11.2 — A punch looks like a punch

As a visitor, I want hits, blocks, landings and KOs to throw real impact art, so
that an exchange reads as a fight rather than as two sprites overlapping.

## Reference material

**Read `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` first.** It gives the reference project's
location (called `<REF>` below), the rule that its path may appear only in
`.md` files, and the four mechanisms that may never be copied.

| What | Where |

## Why this is the first story of the epic

The Ultimate fires once a Match. A hit fires on most Decision Points. Story
10.4's cinematic is the moment people remember, but this is the one that decides
whether the ordinary ninety seconds before it are worth watching.

Today `juice-draw.ts` paints sparks as **squares** — an honest translation
written under the flat-surface rule 11.1 has now lifted for the arena. The
reference ships the real art.

## The assets

`fx_sheet.png` from the reference project, with `atlas/fx.json` describing it:
208x208 cells on a 5x5 grid. Same owner as every other asset in
`apps/web/public/` — `docs/ASSETS.md`'s "author's own prior project" section
settles the provenance question and it is not to be re-opened.

| Pose | Cells | Frames | fps | Fires on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `spark_l` | 0-3 | 4 | 20 | a light hit |
| `spark_h` | 5-8 | 4 | 20 | a heavy hit |
| `block` | 10-12 | 3 | 18 | a blocked hit |
| `dust_puff` | 13-14 | 2 | 12 | a landing |
| `dust` | 15-18 | 4 | 12 | a dash / walk scuff |
| `ko_burst` | 20-24 | 5 | 16 | the KO |
| `slash`, `star`, `streak` | 9, 4, 19 | 1 | — | accents |

**Every pose's cells are contiguous and stay inside one row of the grid**, so
they map onto this repo's existing strip format (`{image, x, y, frames}` with a
fixed `frameWidth`) with no new loader: cell `i` is at
`(i % cols * cellW, floor(i / cols) * cellH)`. That was checked before this
story was written, and it is why this is a small story rather than an atlas
port.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a hit landing, **When** it is drawn, **Then** the impact art for its
grade plays at the contact point — `spark_l` for a light hit, `spark_h` for a
heavy one — advancing one atlas frame per its own tuned frame count, never on a
wall clock (INV-1, INV-3).

**Given** a KO, **When** it lands, **Then** `ko_burst` plays instead of the
ordinary spark. The Match's last hit should not look like its first.

**Given** the timeline scrubber, **When** a visitor seeks across an exchange,
**Then** the effect drawn at a frame index is a pure function of that index:
scrubbing backwards and forwards reproduces identical output. No stepped pool,
no `Math.random()` — scatter comes from `(filmIndex, agentIndex, ordinal, age)`
exactly as Story 9.5's sparks already do.

**Given** the sheet failing to load, **When** the page runs, **Then** the
existing square sparks are drawn instead and nothing throws. Fail-soft, the same
shape `loadArtist` and `loadBackdrop` already use — a fight with no impact art
is worse-looking, never broken.

**Given** the new art, **When** it is composited, **Then** it may use alpha and
additive blending, per Story 11.1.

**Given** any Match, **When** its Final-State Hash is compared before and after,
**Then** it is unchanged (AD-15).

**Given** the new asset, **When** it lands, **Then** `docs/ASSETS.md` records
its provenance and licence **in the same change** — never a later commit.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/render/juice-draw.ts`, `apps/web/src/render/juice.ts` (the tuning
table only — kinds already exist), a small VFX sheet type beside
`render/sprite-sheet.ts`, `apps/web/public/fx/`, `apps/web/src/startup.ts` (one
loader), `docs/ASSETS.md`.

**Note the shape of the change:** `JuiceKind` already grades hits
(`hit`/`heavy`/`ko`) and `JuiceSpark` already carries position, height, size and
age. This story changes what a spark is *drawn as*, not when one exists.

## Visual gate (Story 10.1) — 2026-08-07

Run against `npm run dev` on `localhost:5173` through Chrome DevTools MCP, all
three surfaces, ~2s after load. Console filtered to `error` and `warn`:
**nothing**, across the whole session including the preserved messages from
three navigations — so `loadVfx` did not take its fail-soft path and the sheet
really decoded.

**Two things had to be worked around to see anything at all, and both are worth
recording rather than smoothing over.**

The first is a real trap for every future visual gate on this repo: **headless
Chrome reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`**. Story 9.5 suppresses the
entire juice layer under that preference — no shake, no sparks, and now no
impacts — so the first capture showed a fight with no effects and no warnings,
which is the exact "wrong picture, valid call sequence" failure the gate exists
to catch, arriving from the *harness* rather than from the code. `matchMedia`
was overridden to `no-preference` for the query `prefersReducedMotion` reads,
which is what an ordinary visitor's browser answers. Everything below is under
that override.

The second: the effect is 12 clock frames long, so it cannot be caught by
screenshotting a running page. `CanvasRenderingContext2D.prototype.drawImage`
was hooked to count draws issued while `globalCompositeOperation === 'lighter'`,
the transport was paused, and the timeline was scanned frame by frame to find
one mid-effect.

### Replay player (`#app`)

**The art renders, and it reads as a punch.** 156 additive `drawImage` calls
across the demo Match's 532 clock frames, on 156 distinct clock frames — one
sprite per live impact, exactly as tuned. Captured paused at clock frame 40
(DP 4, Tick 90): source rect `[208, 0, 208, 208]` — `spark_l`, atlas frame 1 —
drawn at `[376, 200, 128, 128]` with `globalAlpha` 1. On screen that is a
bright orange starburst struck at the point the two fighters overlap, at about
chest height on the struck fighter, with Story 9.5's small green scatter
squares still visible around it. **No square was removed**; the flash sits on
top of the debris and under the damage number.

Scanning every frame confirmed the shape: one run of full alpha per impact,
then a fall. Atlas frames advance every 3 clock frames as `holdFrames` says.
The run was 8 frames long when this was first written and is 7 now — see the
second defect below, which is what that extra frame was.

**This gate has now caught two defects in the same five lines of arithmetic,
and both are fixed.**

The first: the ramp's last recorded draw was `alpha: 0.2` — 2000 basis points —
and the sprite then vanished, which is a pop, not a fade. `impactFor` divided
by `remaining` where it needed `remaining - 1`; the test guarding it asserted
only `< FULL`, so it passed against behaviour its own title ("falls to
nothing at the end") contradicted.

The second was found by the review pass that followed, and it is the reason the
sequence written here on the re-run — `1 ×8, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4, 0.2, 0`, thirteen
draws — was arithmetically impossible for a 12-frame impact. The guard read
`remaining >= fadeFrames`, which holds full alpha for one frame too long and
then opens the fade on a **40% drop in a single clock frame**: the real
sequence was `1 ×8, 0.6, 0.4, 0.2, 0`. A smaller pop, in the place a fade is
supposed to be smoothest. Fixed to `remaining > fadeFrames`, and the test now
asserts the whole sequence element by element rather than "monotonic, ends at
zero" — a shape both defects satisfied.

**Re-run on the patched code, and this is a measurement rather than a
restatement of intent.** One landed hit in a driven Arcade Match, scanned frame
by frame over the whole 480-frame timeline: 12 additive draws on 12 consecutive
clock frames (72–83), all `globalCompositeOperation: 'lighter'`, all reading
row `y=0` (`spark_l`) at 128px. Atlas columns `0,0,0,1,1,1,2,2,2,3,3,3` — three
clock frames per cell, as `holdFrames` says. Alpha
`1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4, 0.2, 0` — seven full frames, then five
even steps to nothing. Captured paused at frame 74: a bright orange starburst
struck where the two fighters overlap, at about chest height, with Story 9.5's
green scatter squares around it and the red damage number above. **No square
was removed.**

**Honest gap: only `spark_l` was ever seen on screen.** Every impact drawn in
both available Matches read from row `y=0`. The demo Match and the Arcade
Baseline-Bot fights land only sub-`heavyDamage` hits and both end by TIMEOUT,
so no `heavy` and no `ko` event exists in either to fire `spark_h` or
`ko_burst`. Those two paths are covered by `juice-draw.test.ts` (different
source rects per grade, `ko_burst`'s row for a KO) and by
`vfx-sheet.test.ts`, and the *art* at their offsets was confirmed directly:
all three rows were drawn to a scratch canvas from the shipped PNG and each
holds real, visibly distinct frames — `spark_l` a small four-frame burst,
`spark_h` a much larger yellow star, `ko_burst` a five-frame purple-and-white
explosion. The Match's last hit will not look like its first. But it has not
been watched happening, and that is a gap a later story with a KO-ending log
should close rather than something this gate can claim.

### Arcade (`#arcade`)

Unchanged and unbroken. The panel renders its copy, the Play-vs-CPU button and
the five touch controls; it hosts no canvas of its own — a Match it starts
replays through `#app`. A full Arcade Match was played to completion through
the panel to exercise the re-mount, and **the sheet survived it**: the
re-mounted player scanned to 72 impact frames across 504 clock frames, still
128px `spark_l` sprites, chips reading `Arcade · not rated` / `P2 · TIMEOUT` /
`HASH VERIFIED`. That is `startup.ts`'s `dressing.vfx` slot doing its job —
without it an Arcade Match would have dropped back to bare squares for the rest
of the session.

Captured mid-hit at clock frame 313 with the struck fighter pinned near the
right wall: the sprite is drawn at `dx = 796` on a 960-wide stage and hangs
partly off the edge, clipped by the canvas. That is the intended behaviour and
not an oversight — an impact is struck at a fighter's own position, and sliding
it inward to fit would draw the flash somewhere the hit did not happen. It
reads correctly.

### Spectate (`#spectate`)

Unchanged, as it must be. Backdrop, two visually distinct fighters, both health
bars, both `ULTIMATE READY` gauges, `Now playing spectate-01`, all six entry
buttons. **No impact art, and that is correct**: Spectate has no juice layer at
all yet, so there was nothing for 11.2 to hang a sheet on. Wiring it is Story

### Against `<REF>/shots/04_local_match.png`

The reference shot is a neutral frame with no FX in it, so it settles scale
rather than the effect. Fighters read at ~34% of canvas height here against the
reference's ~32% — close enough that the impact sizes chosen against our
fighters (128px light / 176px heavy / 260px KO on a 960×400 stage) transfer.

The honest difference is **weight**. In the reference these cells are drawn
against a bright, busy stage at a size that fills the space between two
fighters; here a 128px burst on a dark stage reads as a firm, legible hit but
not as the stage-shaking event the reference's arcade feel implies. That is a
tuning number in `DEFAULT_JUICE_TUNING.impactSizePx`, deliberately left
conservative for the first story to land the sheet — a bigger flash on a
960×400 viewport starts covering both fighters at once, and the HUD and stage
work that would carry it is 11.3's and 11.5's. Accepted as-is; recorded so a
later story retunes it on purpose rather than discovering it.

### What shipped, against what this story's first line promises

The sentence at the top asks for "hits, blocks, landings and KOs". **Three
poses shipped — `spark_l`, `spark_h`, `ko_burst` — and `block`, `dust_puff` and
`dust` did not.** Not an oversight and not a cut for time: `JuiceKind` has
exactly three members (`hit`/`heavy`/`ko`) and `deriveJuiceEvents` emits an
event only where damage was dealt. A blocked hit and a landing are not events
this renderer has, and inventing them means new simulation-adjacent derivation,
which this story's scope explicitly forbade. The cells are in the sheet already
and the layout format takes another pose in three lines, so whichever story
adds those events gets the art for free.

There is a matching entry in the loop's deferred-work ledger, but that file
lives under `_bmad-output/`, which `.gitignore` excludes — so it is not in the
repository and does not survive a fresh clone. This paragraph, and the one
below it, are the record that ships. Both gaps in one line each, for whoever
picks them up:

- **Unshipped poses.** `block`, `dust_puff` and `dust` are cells 10–19 of the
  sheet with no `JuiceKind` to hang them on; adding one means a new event in
  `deriveJuiceEvents`, not a new pose in `layout.json`.
- **Unwatched grades.** `spark_h` and `ko_burst` have no reachable Match that
  fires them (below).

The related gap: **`spark_h` and `ko_burst` have never been watched happening.**
The demo Match and the Arcade Baseline-Bot fights land only sub-`heavyDamage`
hits and both end by TIMEOUT, so no reachable surface contains a `heavy` or a
`ko` event. Both paths are covered by test, and all three rows were drawn from
the shipped PNG onto a scratch canvas in the live browser to confirm the art
itself is distinct (`spark_l` a small four-frame burst, `spark_h` a larger
yellow star, `ko_burst` a five-frame purple-and-white explosion) — but that is
not the same as seeing one fire in a fight, and it is deferred rather than
claimed.

## Visual gate re-run (third review pass) — 2026-08-07

Re-run because that pass changed what is on screen: `paintImpacts` now forces
`imageSmoothingEnabled = false`. Same method as above, on `localhost:5179`, with
the same two workarounds — headless Chrome still reports
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and the effect is still too short to catch by
screenshotting a running page.

**The defect was real and the gate is what proved it.** With `drawImage` hooked,
the ambient `imageSmoothingEnabled` at the moment the juice layer runs reads
`true` — `artist.ts` and `backdrop.ts` each set it `false` inside their *own*
`save`/`restore` pair, so the flag is back at the canvas default by the time the
impact sprite is drawn. Every one of this story's tests passed while a 208px
pixel-art cell was being resampled bilinear down to 128 (`hit`) and up to 260
(`ko`); no recording fake in the suite reads the flag, so nothing could see it.
The sprite now reads with hard pixel edges against the fighters.

Captured on all three surfaces, console filtered to `error` and `warn`:
**nothing**.

- **Replay player** — clock frame 39 of the demo Match, mid-burst. 156 additive
  draws across 156 distinct clock frames, unchanged from the previous run. One
  burst reads `sy = 0` (the `spark_l` row) with `sx` stepping
  `0 → 208 → 416 → 624` every three clock frames, which is `holdFrames: 3` from
  the shipped layout, and alpha stepping `1 ×7, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4, 0.2, 0` — the
  sequence the docblock claims, element for element, over exactly 12 frames.
  Every draw reports `globalCompositeOperation: 'lighter'` and
  `imageSmoothingEnabled: false`. The scatter squares are still there above the
  sprite; no square was removed.
- **Arcade** — a live Baseline-Bot fight, driven to contact through the on-screen
  buttons: 24 sheet draws on the re-mounted player, same row, same cells,
  smoothing off. This is the first direct evidence that `startup.ts`'s re-mount
  adoption of `dressing.vfx` works on the real page rather than only in a test;
  the previous pass shipped that path with no coverage at all, and this pass
  added the assertions in `startup.test.ts` alongside this observation.
- **Spectate** — renders normally and is untouched, as it must be: it has no
  juice layer yet, and wiring one is Story 11.6.

## Standing rule — translate, do not reimplement

The reference draws these through a stepped particle pool with its own RNG. Do
not port the pool. A stepped pool cannot be scrubbed backwards and an ungoverned
RNG contradicts the claim that a replay reproduces. Take the **art and the frame
counts**; express them in this project's precomputed, frame-indexed juice track.

If this story appears to require a change to anything in `docs/contracts/`,
**stop and escalate**.
