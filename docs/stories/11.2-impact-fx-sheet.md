# Story 11.2 — A punch looks like a punch

As a visitor, I want hits, blocks, landings and KOs to throw real impact art, so
that an exchange reads as a fight rather than as two sprites overlapping.

## Reference material

**Read `docs/DEV-REFERENCE.md` first.** It gives the reference project's
location (called `<REF>` below), the rule that its path may appear only in
`.md` files, and the four mechanisms that may never be copied.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Atlas to copy | `<REF>/game_assets/atlas/fx.json` |
| Image to copy | `<REF>/game_assets/fx/fx_sheet.png` — 208×208 cells, 5×5 grid |
| How the reference spawns these | `<REF>/game_source/js/vfx.js:42-98` — **the particle pool. Do not port it.** |
| Hitstop / shake / flash | `<REF>/game_source/js/vfx.js:232` — `feel` |

Copy the image into `apps/web/public/fx/` and write the strip layout yourself.
**Nothing under `apps/web/public/` may name the reference project**, in a
filename or in contents — copied bytes are fine, the name is not.

## Why this is the first story of the epic

The Ultimate fires once a Match. A hit fires on most Decision Points. Story
10.4's cinematic is the moment people remember, but this is the one that decides
whether the ordinary ninety seconds before it are worth watching.

Today `juice-draw.ts` paints sparks as **squares** — an honest translation
written under the flat-surface rule 11.1 has now lifted for the arena. The
reference ships the real art.

## The assets

`fx_sheet.png` from the reference project, with `atlas/fx.json` describing it:
208x208 cells on a 5x5 grid. Same owner as every other asset in
`apps/web/public/` — `docs/ASSETS.md`'s "author's own prior project" section
settles the provenance question and it is not to be re-opened.

| Pose | Cells | Frames | fps | Fires on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `spark_l` | 0-3 | 4 | 20 | a light hit |
| `spark_h` | 5-8 | 4 | 20 | a heavy hit |
| `block` | 10-12 | 3 | 18 | a blocked hit |
| `dust_puff` | 13-14 | 2 | 12 | a landing |
| `dust` | 15-18 | 4 | 12 | a dash / walk scuff |
| `ko_burst` | 20-24 | 5 | 16 | the KO |
| `slash`, `star`, `streak` | 9, 4, 19 | 1 | — | accents |

**Every pose's cells are contiguous and stay inside one row of the grid**, so
they map onto this repo's existing strip format (`{image, x, y, frames}` with a
fixed `frameWidth`) with no new loader: cell `i` is at
`(i % cols * cellW, floor(i / cols) * cellH)`. That was checked before this
story was written, and it is why this is a small story rather than an atlas
port.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a hit landing, **When** it is drawn, **Then** the impact art for its
grade plays at the contact point — `spark_l` for a light hit, `spark_h` for a
heavy one — advancing one atlas frame per its own tuned frame count, never on a
wall clock (INV-1, INV-3).

**Given** a KO, **When** it lands, **Then** `ko_burst` plays instead of the
ordinary spark. The Match's last hit should not look like its first.

**Given** the timeline scrubber, **When** a visitor seeks across an exchange,
**Then** the effect drawn at a frame index is a pure function of that index:
scrubbing backwards and forwards reproduces identical output. No stepped pool,
no `Math.random()` — scatter comes from `(filmIndex, agentIndex, ordinal, age)`
exactly as Story 9.5's sparks already do.

**Given** the sheet failing to load, **When** the page runs, **Then** the
existing square sparks are drawn instead and nothing throws. Fail-soft, the same
shape `loadArtist` and `loadBackdrop` already use — a fight with no impact art
is worse-looking, never broken.

**Given** the new art, **When** it is composited, **Then** it may use alpha and
additive blending, per Story 11.1.

**Given** any Match, **When** its Final-State Hash is compared before and after,
**Then** it is unchanged (AD-15).

**Given** the new asset, **When** it lands, **Then** `docs/ASSETS.md` records
its provenance and licence **in the same change** — never a later commit.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/render/juice-draw.ts`, `apps/web/src/render/juice.ts` (the tuning
table only — kinds already exist), a small VFX sheet type beside
`render/sprite-sheet.ts`, `apps/web/public/fx/`, `apps/web/src/startup.ts` (one
loader), `docs/ASSETS.md`.

**Note the shape of the change:** `JuiceKind` already grades hits
(`hit`/`heavy`/`ko`) and `JuiceSpark` already carries position, height, size and
age. This story changes what a spark is *drawn as*, not when one exists.

## Invariants at risk

- **AD-15** — hash-neutral, asserted not assumed.
- **INV-1 / INV-3** — frames, never milliseconds. The atlas's own `fps` numbers
  are a *description of the source art*, not a playback clock: convert them to
  frame counts at load and never read a clock.
- **INV-8** — same-origin static asset. No CDN; the site must look identical
  offline.

## Test plan

- A light hit and a heavy hit select different poses.
- A KO selects `ko_burst`.
- The same frame index drawn twice yields the same call sequence.
- Seeking backwards and forwards across an exchange reproduces identical frames.
- With no sheet loaded, the square-spark path draws and nothing throws.
- Final-State Hash unchanged with the sheet present and absent.

## Exit gate

`npm test` green **AND** `bash scripts/audit-invariants.sh` exiting zero **AND**
`docs/ASSETS.md` updated in the same change **AND** Story 10.1's visual gate,
with a landed hit captured mid-effect on all three surfaces and the finding
recorded here.

## Standing rule — translate, do not reimplement

The reference draws these through a stepped particle pool with its own RNG. Do
not port the pool. A stepped pool cannot be scrubbed backwards and an ungoverned
RNG contradicts the claim that a replay reproduces. Take the **art and the frame
counts**; express them in this project's precomputed, frame-indexed juice track.

If this story appears to require a change to anything in `docs/contracts/`,
**stop and escalate**.
