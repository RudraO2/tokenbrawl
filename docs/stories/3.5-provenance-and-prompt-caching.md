# Story 3.5 — Per-call provenance and prompt caching

As the developer, I want every call attributed and the shared prefix cached, so that we rank deployments honestly and free quotas last long enough to finish a tournament.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** any Deployment Decision entry, **When** the log is written, **Then** it carries the `provider` and `endpoint` that served **that specific call** — a mid-Match fallback must be visible.

**Given** two endpoints serving the same model name, **When** the leaderboard is generated, **Then** they occupy two distinct rows.

**Given** a Match, **When** it completes, **Then** cache-hit rate over the Match is measured and reported.

**Given** a provider that reports cached-token counts, **When** the bank is debited, **Then** cached tokens are excluded.

**Given** a provider that does **not** report cache signal, **When** the bank is debited, **Then** debiting is conservative — cached tokens are charged — and the deviation is logged per Deployment and published.

## Scope

`packages/providers` and `packages/core`: provenance plumbing and per-provider caching. Cache mechanics differ per vendor and change often; verify current behaviour before implementing each.

## Frozen contracts touched

None modified — the schema already carries per-entry `provider` and `endpoint`.

## Invariants at risk

- **INV-6** — provenance per call.
- **INV-4** — cache handling must not become a hidden way of setting effort.

## Architecture decisions that constrain this

- **AD-11** — provenance per call; two endpoints are two rows.
- **AD-6** — the Harness owns the debit, including the cached-token exclusion.

## Test plan

Per-call provenance under a simulated mid-Match fallback. Two-endpoints-two-rows. Cache-hit measurement. Conservative-debit path with its published deviation note.

## Exit gate

`npm test` green **AND** `bash scripts/audit-invariants.sh` exiting zero. Reviewer opinion is never a gate.
