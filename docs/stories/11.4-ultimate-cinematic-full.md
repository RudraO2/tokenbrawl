# Story 11.4 — The Ultimate becomes a cutscene

As a visitor, I want the Ultimate to build, detonate and then fire, so that it
lands like the arcade super it is modelled on rather than like an event that has
already happened.

## What is wrong with the cinematic we have

Story 10.4 shipped a correct cinematic under the flat-surface rule: a 90-frame
renderer-held freeze, a solid flash on frames 0-3, an accent band that expands
over 22 frames and holds, a streak field, a shake, and an `ULTIMATE` banner.
Every part of it is hash-neutral and seek-safe, and **that machinery is kept.**

What is wrong is the **timing**. Ours is front-loaded — the flash is the first
thing that happens. The reference is back-loaded, and that is why its version
reads as a payoff:

| | Reference | Ours today |
|---|---|---|
| Flash | tick 80, at the end | frames 0-3, at the start |
| Build | orb 6px → 80px on `growP²` across ticks 0-78, then blooms ×3.2 | none |
| Release | 50-tick beam sweeping out on `easeOutCubic` over 8 ticks | band expands 22 frames, holds |
| Framing | letterbox bars in over 15 ticks (`easeOutBack`), vignette | none |
| Subject | painted portrait slides in from the caster's side | text banner |

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** the Ultimate's freeze, **When** it plays, **Then** it runs as three
acts: a **build** during which an energy orb at the caster's hand grows on an
accelerating curve and blooms into a muzzle flash; a **slam**, a full-frame
flash tinted with the caster's aura, near the end of the freeze; and a
**release**, a beam that sweeps out from the caster toward the target.

**Given** the freeze, **When** it opens, **Then** letterbox bars slide in over a
tuned frame count and a vignette darkens the stage, so the frame reads as a
cutscene rather than as a fight with a banner over it.

**Given** the caster, **When** the cinematic draws them, **Then** it draws
**their own** portrait and **their own** `ult_<id>_muzzle` / `_beam` / `_impact`
art in their own aura colour. Per the owner's ruling of 2026-08-07 the Ultimate's
*presentation* is per-character.

**Given** that per-character presentation, **When** the simulation is examined,
**Then** it is untouched: one `special` Action, one cost, one damage number, one
frame window, for every fighter. This story adds art and colour, never a
mechanic — no `packages/env-fighter` change whatsoever.

**Given** a fighter with no portrait or no ult art, **When** they throw it,
**Then** the cinematic degrades to the shared set and then to Story 10.4's
banner-and-band, and nothing throws.

**Given** every duration and extent, **When** they are expressed, **Then** they
are integer frame counts and basis points in the tuning table, never
milliseconds, never a clock, and never a literal at a call site (INV-1, INV-3).

**Given** the timeline scrubber, **When** a visitor seeks across the Ultimate,
**Then** every frame is a pure function of the frame index — scrubbing back and
forth reproduces identical output.

**Given** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, **When** the Ultimate plays,
**Then** the track keeps its shape and frame count, and the flash, the shake and
the moving field are switched off, exactly as Story 10.4 already does.

**Given** any Match, **When** its Final-State Hash is compared before and after,
**Then** it is unchanged (AD-15). **This is the story's central risk and it is
asserted against `spectate-03`**, the one committed Command Log containing an
Ultimate — the demo Match contains none and would pass vacuously.

**Given** the new assets, **When** they land, **Then** `docs/ASSETS.md` records
them **in the same change**.

## Standing rule — translate, do not reimplement

Repeated from Story 10.4 because it is still the likeliest way this goes wrong,
and 11.1 has removed the guard rail that used to make it obvious:

- **The reference's `CINEMATIC_FREEZE = 90` lives inside its `step()`.** It
  freezes the *simulation*. Porting that changes Tick counts, Match length, the
  Decision Point budget and **every Final-State Hash in this repository**. The
  freeze stays on the clock axis, held by the renderer.
- **Do not port the particle pool.** It is stepped and it draws from an
  ungoverned RNG. Both contradict a replay that reproduces.
- **Do not port the wall clocks.** No `setTimeout`, no `performance.now()`, no
  `ctx.currentTime`.

What is fair game is the **behavioural facts**: 90 frames of held picture,
letterbox over ~15 with `easeOutBack`, orb `6→80px` on `growP²` to ~tick 78,
bloom ×3.2 over ticks 66-78, flash at ~80, beam sweeping over 8 with
`easeOutCubic`, impact art at 1.8x the beam scale.

## Test plan

- Final-State Hash identical with the cinematic enabled and disabled, on
  `spectate-03`.
- The same frame index drawn twice yields the same call sequence.
- Seeking backwards across the Ultimate and forwards again reproduces frames.
- A Match with no Ultimate produces an unchanged call sequence.
- Two fighters produce two different cinematics (per-character art is wired, not
  merely present — the Story 9.7 failure).
- A missing portrait falls back without throwing.
