# Story 12.4 — One screen at a time, and it fits on a phone

As a visitor, I want the site to be an arcade cabinet with screens I move between, so that I am not scrolling past four stacked panels of a page that runs off the side of my phone.

## The gap, measured

`apps/web/index.html` is five sibling sections in document order: `#landing`, `#app`, `#byok`, `#arcade`, `#spectate`. Every one of them is mounted and running at once, all the time. There is no screen concept, no route, and no way to be *on* the arcade rather than scrolled to it. Story 9.8's landing CTAs are `scrollTo('#arcade')` calls, which is the honest expression of what the page is.

Two consequences the gate measured on 2026-08-10:

- **The page scrolls sideways on a phone.** At 390×844, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is **992** against a `clientWidth` of **390**. The 960px arena canvas does not scale, and `.tb-tagline` runs to **409px**. `no-horizontal-overflow` is waived in `docs/visual/known-failures.json` naming this story.
- **Three canvases animate simultaneously**, because Spectate loops forever whether or not anyone is looking at it and the player autoplays on load. That is three render loops and three audio graphs competing on a phone, which is also why Story 12.1's note records the hero determinism test timing out against a browser left rendering at 60fps.

The reference is a cabinet: `<REF>/shots/03_local_title.png` is a title screen with a roster and `INSERT COIN`, and `04_local_match.png` is a match screen filling the viewport. You are on one or the other. That is the target.

## What the router is

A **screen router over the sections that already exist**, not a rewrite of them. `#landing`, `#app`, `#arcade`, `#spectate` and `#byok` stay exactly the panels they are, with the mount functions `startup.ts` already calls. The router owns three things and nothing else:

1. **Which screen is showing.** Exactly one, filling the viewport.
2. **What a hidden screen is allowed to do.** A screen that is not showing does not paint and does not make sound. This is the point of the story that is not about layout: it is what makes the page cheap enough to run on a phone.
3. **How you got there.** A hash route (`#/play`, `#/watch`, `#/byok`) so a screen is linkable and the back button works. No history rewriting, no server, no framework — `INV-8` and `docs/DESIGN.md`'s "no CSS framework, no runtime dependency" both still hold. `apps/web` carries `vite` and `vitest` and nothing else.

**The arena canvas becomes responsive here.** Its backbuffer stays 960×400 — playback is resolution-independent by design and every renderer assertion depends on it — and CSS scales the element to the viewport with the aspect ratio preserved. `max-width: 100%` on the canvas box is most of the fix; `.tb-tagline`'s 409px is the rest.

**A screen for character select is reserved but not built here.** Story 12.5 builds it. This story leaves the route registered and the check for it waived, so that 12.5 has a place to land rather than a shell to renegotiate.

## Acceptance criteria

**Given** a visitor on a 390×844 phone, **When** the page loads and when each screen is shown, **Then** `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= clientWidth`, **and** `no-horizontal-overflow` passes with its entry deleted from `docs/visual/known-failures.json`.

**Given** any viewport, **When** the gate loads the page, **Then** exactly one screen section is visible and the others have zero-area bounding boxes — the check `one-screen-at-a-time`, added to `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` by this story.

**Given** a screen that is not showing, **When** the gate samples for a second later, **Then** its canvas has not repainted and no audio source has started on it — the check `hidden-screens-are-idle`, which hashes every off-screen canvas 700ms apart and requires the hash unchanged. This is `spectate-animates` inverted, and both must hold at once: the shown surface animates, the hidden ones do not.

**Given** a visitor who clicks `Play vs CPU` from the landing screen, **When** the route changes, **Then** the arcade screen is showing, its canvas is visible and live (Story 12.2's `arcade-live-canvas` and `arcade-input-moves-fighter` still pass), and the URL carries the route.

**Given** a visitor who reloads on `#/watch`, **When** the page comes back, **Then** they are on the same screen. A route that does not survive a reload is a tab strip.

**Given** the browser's back button, **When** pressed after two navigations, **Then** it returns to the previous screen rather than leaving the site.

**Given** every interactive control the router adds, **When** focused, **Then** it carries the visible 3px `--tb-accent` outline `docs/DESIGN.md` requires, and every screen is reachable by keyboard alone.

## Scope

`apps/web/index.html`, a new `apps/web/src/shell/` (the router and the screen registry), `apps/web/src/startup.ts` (mounting through the router rather than all at once), `apps/web/src/styles/` (the shell's layout and the responsive canvas box), `apps/web/src/landing/panel.ts` (its CTAs become routes rather than scrolls), `scripts/visual-gate.mjs` (three checks; the existing per-surface captures must now navigate to a screen before capturing), `docs/visual/known-failures.json` (delete `no-horizontal-overflow`, add `character-select-reachable`).

Not in scope: what any panel draws inside itself, the HUD (12.6), audio defaults (12.9). The gate itself must keep capturing all three rendering surfaces every run — a router that made a surface unreachable to the gate would have hidden the next Spectate regression rather than fixed a layout.

## Frozen contracts touched

None.

## Invariants at risk

- **INV-8** — static hosting only. A hash route is served by the same static file; a path route would need a server rewrite and is therefore out.
- **AD-17** — Spectate stays a manifest walk over committed logs. Pausing it when hidden must not turn it into something computed on demand when shown; `offsetForNow` still decides where a visitor joins.
- **INV-3** — `loopStartEpochMs` stays a fixed anchor constant.

## Two regimes

Everything this story touches is **page chrome** and keeps every rule in `docs/DESIGN.md`: 3–4px solid `--tb-ink` borders, `6px 6px 0` hard shadow with zero blur, `border-radius: 0`, flat fills, press-displaces interaction, stepped `--tb-step` motion or none. The arena's release covers `apps/web/src/render/**/*.ts` only — a screen transition is not the arena and does not get a gradient. Read the "Two regimes" section before writing any CSS.

## Test plan

- The router shows exactly one screen for every route, including an unknown one (which lands on the landing screen rather than on a blank page).
- A hidden screen's clock is stopped and its audio sink receives nothing.
- A shown screen's clock is running.
- Back and forward move through the screen history.
- The layout suite asserts no element exceeds the viewport at 390px.

**The checks were mutation-tested rather than assumed.** Two mutations, each re-running the whole
gate:

- Replacing every screen's `onHide` with a no-op: `hidden-screens-are-idle-{desktop,mobile}` both
  FAIL with `REPAINTED: app`.
- Reverting `shown` to an empty set in `shell/router.ts` — the exact shape of the bug the review
  found: `hidden-screens-are-idle-on-load-{desktop,mobile}` both FAIL with
  `REPAINTED: app, spectate`, while the later sample stays green, which is the whole reason the
  on-load sample exists.

**One gate defect found and fixed while measuring, and it is not small.** Headless Chrome answers
`matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches` with `true`, and
`--force-prefers-reduced-motion=0` does not change it — the Chromium switch is presence-only, so
passing `=0` reads as passing the flag. Verified with `--dump-dom` against a page that prints the
query, with the flag and without: `true` both times. **Every run of this gate since Story 12.1 has
measured the reduced-motion product**, in which the replay clock emits one frame and stops, Spectate
declines to start its stream, and the juice layer drops the shake and the particles. An idle-canvas
check would have passed on a page with no router in it at all. `Emulation.setEmulatedMedia` with
`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` is the lever that works, and the gate now sets it.

**And one capture defect.** `scrollIntoView` on a section taller than the viewport centres on
whatever is in its middle, which at 390px on `#app` is the reasoning panel — Story 12.3's finding had
to record that its mobile replay capture showed no arena. Captures now scroll the surface's *canvas*
into view when it has one. A capture that does not show the thing it is named after is not evidence.

## Independent review — what was fixed, and what was not

An independent review of the diff raised nine findings. Six were fixed; three are recorded here.

**Fixed.**

- **The router hid nothing on its first apply (HIGH).** `apply` fired `onHide` only for the screen
  just left, and on the first apply there is no such screen. Every panel mounts *running* — the
  replay player autoplays, the Spectate stream starts at mount — so a fresh load of `/` showed the
  landing screen while `#app` and `#spectate` painted at 60fps behind `display: none`. That is
  verbatim the defect this story exists to fix, shipped inside the fix for it. `shown` now starts
  holding every route, which is the true state of the page, so the first apply stops all but one.
- **The unit test that should have caught it was written against the behaviour.**
  `router.test.ts`'s case named *"stops the clocks of every screen it is not showing, on the very
  first apply"* asserted `['show /play']` — i.e. it pinned the absence of the behaviour in its own
  name. It now asserts the hides, and fails on the defect.
- **The gate could not have caught it either.** Its only idle sample was taken after the run had
  already left every screen once, which is the sole path on which `onHide` fired.
  `hidden-screens-are-idle-on-load` samples before the first navigation, which is the state a visitor
  arrives in.
- **A hidden screen kept making sound.** Pausing the Spectate walk leaves its looping music bed
  running, and the player's bed is a looping source begun at clock frame 0 that outlives the clock
  that started it. Spectate now goes through its own audio toggle in both directions, so a returning
  visitor gets their bed back; the player's graph is stopped outright. This also forced `apply` into
  two passes — every `onHide` before any `onShow` — because the page has *one* audio graph, and in
  registry order the outgoing screen's teardown ran after the incoming screen's setup and silenced
  the surface that had just claimed the buses. Pinned by a case that puts `/watch` before `/replay`
  in the registry, which is the real order and the one that produced it.
- **A finished Match force-navigated the visitor from wherever they were.** An arcade Match keeps
  running while its screen is hidden by design, so one a visitor had walked away from would finish on
  its own and yank them off Spectate mid-watch. It also made the gate order-dependent. `mount` now
  navigates only from the screen that started the Match (`/play` or `/byok`).
- **`no-horizontal-overflow` never measured a live arcade stage at 390px**, and the duplicated
  `SCREEN_ROUTES` list in the gate had no drift guard despite its comment claiming one. Both fixed:
  the gate starts a mobile Match before measuring, and `one-screen-at-a-time` compares the page's own
  `[data-screen]` count against the gate's route list. The back button is now driven through
  `history.back()`/`forward()` in a real browser rather than only through a fake history in the unit
  tests.

**Declined — the mobile `hidden-screens-are-idle` sample has one vacuous half.** The review noted
that on mobile the hidden `#arcade` canvas was idle regardless of the router, because no Match had
been started. That is no longer true — the mobile overflow fix starts one — but the general point
stands for any surface a run never activates. Not chased further: the check names every canvas it
sampled and every canvas that moved, so a reader can see what it measured, and the pausing of the
arcade view specifically is covered by four cases in `arcade/live.test.ts` that assert the arena
makes *no drawing call at all* while paused, including the two paints that do not go through the
clock (the synchronous first frame, and a sprite pack decoding mid-Match).

**Declined — resuming Spectate continues from where it paused rather than re-deriving from
`offsetForNow` (AD-17).** The invariant says pausing must not turn Spectate into something computed
on demand when shown, and it is not: `offsetForNow` still decides where a visitor *joins* the
manifest walk, exactly as before, and nothing about the walk is recomputed on show. What changes is
that a visitor who leaves and returns is no longer in lockstep with the shared wall clock. That is
the necessary cost of the story's own second acceptance criterion — a stream that kept walking while
hidden would be the defect — and re-seeking to `offsetForNow` on every show would make the back
button skip the fight forward, which is worse. **Deferred: whether returning to Watch should re-join
the live stream or resume**, owed by `12-11`, which regenerates the corpus and is where the stream's
continuity is decided.

**Declined — a route change moves no focus and announces nothing.** The acceptance criterion is
*"every screen is reachable by keyboard alone"*, and it is: the nav is six anchors, tab-reachable,
each with the 3px `--tb-accent` focus ring `docs/DESIGN.md` requires. Moving focus to the new screen's
heading and announcing the change through a live region is real and separate work — it needs a
heading to move focus *to* on every screen, which `#app` and `#select` have and `#play`'s live arena
does not. **Deferred: focus management and route announcement**, owed by `12-6`, which is the story
that builds the match screen's furniture and is where the heading it would land on comes from.

**Deferred, and not from the review: the match screen is not full-bleed.** See the reference
comparison above. Owed by `12-6`.

**Deferred: the replay player's music bed does not come back.** `onHide` stops the page's audio
graph, and the bed's cue sits at clock frame 0, so a visitor returning to `#/replay` mid-film gets
the fight back without its music. Re-arming it needs a verb that does not exist — the director has
`atFrame`, not "resume the bed". Owed by `12-9`, which owns audio defaults.

**Recorded: `npm test` failed once, in `packages/env-fighter`, and did not reproduce.** It failed on
the first full run while the visual gate's Chrome was still winding down on the same machine, and
passed on the two runs after it and on every run since (264/264 alone, 264/264 in the fanned-out
suite). Same class as Story 12.1's note about the hero determinism test timing out against a browser
left rendering at 60fps. Worth knowing before assuming a red `env-fighter` is a real regression.

## Standing rule

If this story appears to require a change to anything in `docs/contracts/`, or a server, a worker or a build-time route table, **stop and escalate** — the last of those means the static-hosting design was misread.
